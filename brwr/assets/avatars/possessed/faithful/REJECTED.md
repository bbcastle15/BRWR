Spell badge; Revenge of the Thorn's Power/Trophy pictograms). They must not be
loaded or exported as production cards. The directory name is the attempted goal,
not a certification. Original screenshots remain in the parent directory.

rejected production/hd/restored redesigns. HD restoration is explicitly incomplete.
