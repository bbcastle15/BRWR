A game in Apocalypse Mode uses all the standard rules of the

FEATS
In Apocalypse mode, each Mage has Feats, listed on their Mage 
Card. A Feat changes certain rules of the game for the Mage 
who has it. The list of Feats and their detailed explanation can 
be found on page 9.
LIBRARY
There is no shared Library; instead, each player has their own 
Personal Library, consisting solely of the deck obtained by the 
Mage during step 9 of the Setup.
Apocalypse Game Rules
Any Eff ect that would direct a Mage to draw cards from the 
Library instead causes that Mage to draw cards from their 
Personal Library. Eff ects that allow Forgotten Spell Cards to be 
drawn, on the other hand, follow the standard rules.

used freely in Apocalypse Mode.
