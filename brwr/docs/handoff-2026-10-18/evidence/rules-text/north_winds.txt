Mechanics rules: Transformation
Some Spell Cards allow you to perform a Transformation as part of one of their Eff ects. 
A Transformation is identifi ed by the Transform keyword followed by the name of the 
Transformation.
When a Mage performs Transform, they follow these steps:
• They replace their Mage's Model with the Model of the indicated Transformation, if 
available; otherwise, they ignore the Transformation.
• They discard all their Equipment Evocations.
• They place the  that transformed them next to their Mage Sheet, rotated as indicated 
by the Transform Eff ect.
As long as that  remains next to their Mage Sheet, the Mage is considered Transformed and the 
Physical Actions listed on that  are available to that Mage. When it is in that position, that  is 
considered the  that Transformed the Mage.
The Mage discards the  that transformed them when:
• They are defeated.
• They are about to perform Transform.
When this happens, that Mage is no longer Transformed and must replace the Model of 
their Transformation with the Model of their Mage.
At the Beginning of the Evocations Phase, in play order, a Transformed Mage may perform one 
of the Physical Actions listed on the  that transformed them without exhausting a .
