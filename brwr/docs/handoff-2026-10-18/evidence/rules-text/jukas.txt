Jukas is an iconic character from the Nova Aetas 
Chronicles universe, known as a skilled spellcaster 
capable of traveling through time and practicing 
teleportation. By choosing Jukas as the Avatar, 
players will be confronted by this character's subtle 
art of manipulating space and time, combined 
with the power of the Black Rose.

Avatar Special Rules — Jukas
VORTEX TOKEN () 
Some of the Eff ects related to Jukas place a Vortex 
Token in a Room. A Room may contain a single 
Vortex Token. If an Eff ect indicates to place a Vortex 
Token in a Room that already has one, or indicates 
to place one but no Vortex Tokens are present in the 
game pool, no Vortex Token is placed. 
Vortex Tokens follow these rules:
• Resolved Spell: after a Mage (other than Jukas) has resolved 
the Eff ect of one of their Spells while in a Room that contains 
a Vortex Token, they must take that token and place it on their 
Mage Card. A Vortex Token that is on a Mage Card is not 
considered to be in the Lodge. 
• Clean-up Phase: at the end of Step 2 of the Clean-up Phase, in 
play order, Mages must discard all the Vortex Tokens they have 
from their Mage Card. If a Mage discards two or more Vortex 
Tokens in this way, they draw a Forgotten Spell.
Apply the following steps during Step 3 of the Avatar 
Setup:
• Clip the Avatar Base to the Jukas Model.
• Place the Model of Jukas in the Throne Room.
• Place a  Vortex Token in the Throne Room and in 
each Room adjacent to it.
