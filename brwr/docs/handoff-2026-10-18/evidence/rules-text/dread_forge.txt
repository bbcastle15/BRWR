ETNA SETUP
When used as an Avatar, Etna cannot be chosen as a Mage during Step 8 of the Setup.
Apply the following steps during Step 3 of the Avatar Setup:
• Place the Forge Room so that it is adjacent to the Black Rose Room.
• Clip the Large Avatar Base to the Etna Model.
• Place the Etna Model in the Forge Room.
• Clip the Avatar’s Large Evocation Base to the Magma Model.
• Place the Magma Model in the Black Rose Room.
• Place the Magma Evocation Card on the  Slot of the Avatar Board.
• Place a Glyph Token  in each Room adjacent to the Black Rose Room.
• Remove the Lava Flow card from the Forgotten Spells deck.

GLYPH TOKENS 
The Glyph Tokens in the Lodge can 
be collected by Mages to exploit their 
devastating energy.
During the game, Etna will try to collect and 
engrave Glyphs to enhance his Evocation, 
Magma.
Glyph Tokens follow these rules:
• Collect: when a Mage activates a Room in 
which there is at least one Glyph Token, 
if that Mage is in that Room, they obtain
a Glyph Token from that Room (and not 
from a Mage Card) and place it on their 
Mage Card (Avatar Card for Etna) showing 
its Active side (see image). A Glyph Token 
that is on a Mage Card is considered to be 
in the same Room as that Mage.
• Defeat: when a Mage (including Etna) 
defeats another Mage who has at least 
one Glyph Token on their Mage Card
