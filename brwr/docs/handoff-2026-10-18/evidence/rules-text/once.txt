Yellow Brand Token to the target Mage.

following rules:

to a Mage is considered to be in the Lodge. 
When a Yellow Brand Token is discarded, 
it goes back into the shared pool.
• Actions: a Mage who has a Yellow Brand 
Token on their Mage Card can use the 
Introspection Physical Action (see box).

as indicated below for each one:
