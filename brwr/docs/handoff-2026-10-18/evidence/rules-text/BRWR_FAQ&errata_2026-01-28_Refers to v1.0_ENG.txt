FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Q: Questions — A: Answers
AVATAR DEFEATED AND ADDITIONAL 
CARD
Q.
• What happens if the Avatar is defeated, for example due 
to a Trap, while they are resolving the top Effect of a 
Command Card?
• If the second Effect of the Command Card is a Spell, is 
it ignored because the Avatar is in their Cell?
• If an Avatar is defeated while resolving an Effect and 
that Effect shows the Additional Card symbol, does the 
Avatar have to reveal the additional card?
• If the top Effect of the additional card (in case it must 
be revealed) shows a Spell must it be resolved even 
if the Avatar is in their own Cell? Is this Spell Effect 
considered an integral part of the previous Effect that 
contained the additional card symbol or is it considered 
a new "Cast a Spell" Action?
A.
• The Avatar continues to resolve the Effect up to 
the period (.), then the Avatar's defeat is resolved. 
Finally, the Avatar continues to resolve the Effect 
they were resolving at the time they were defeated. 
Then they attempt to perform the lower Effect of 
the same Command Card, but this effect is ignored 
because the Avatar is located in their own Cell. The 
Avatar never performs Momentum or Explore Actions.
Note that any Trap/Protect Effects of the Avatar resolve 
even if they are placed in the Cell, as with any other 
Mage.
• Yes, like any other Mage, if the Avatar is in their Cell, 
they cannot Cast a Spell. For the Avatar, the Spell Effect 
of a Command Card is equivalent to a Spell Casting 
Action for a player-controlled Mage. 
• Yes, the additional card must be revealed. (No rule 
prohibits resolving the Additional Card symbol, even if 
no Avatar can perform Momentum or Explore).
• The answer in step 2 applies. The Avatar reveals the 
Additional Card, but Spell Effects cannot be performed 
as long as the Avatar is in the Cell. It is not considered 
part of the previous Effect.
AVATAR DEFEAT, DOMINION
Q. Does the Possessed Avatar's Domination skill only apply 
if they are in their Cell at the start of their activation, or 
does it apply anytime they must resolve an Effect outside 
of their activation?
A. The Dominion skill applies only at the beginning of their 
activation.

HEAL
Q. If a Heal effect targets a Room, are all models in the 
Room healed, both my Mage and other players' Mages 
and Evocations?
A. Yes.
EMPTY DECK, LIBRARY, EVENT, QUESTS 
(page 14)
Q. What happens if in the Study Phase I want to draw a Spell 
Card from a school but that school's deck is empty?
A. As a general rule, when the player is to draw/reveal/add/
search a card from an empty deck, all the discards from 
that deck must be shuffled. Then the player can resume 
the Effect using the new deck. This applies to school 
decks in the Library, a Mage's Grimoire, the Event deck, 
and the Quest deck.
TASK, QUEST OR JINX (page 12)
Q. It is mandatory to place the Cube on a Quest or Jinx Card 
when my Mage fulfills the condition of a Task?
A. Yes, it is mandatory.
EVOCATIONS, VALUES WITH ASTERISK (*)
Q. What happens if an Effect targets an Evocation with a 
Health value of 3 or less, but that Evocation has only an 
Asterisk (*) printed on its Health value, without any other 
number?
A. In BRW there are Evocation Cards with Health values 
indicated only by an Asterisk (*). Those values are neither 
greater nor less than any number, so they are never taken 
into account by Effects that target a Model based on 
that value. These Effects therefore cannot be applied to 
Models in the game that show only an Asterisk (*) value 
with no other values.
However, if a value shows a number together with an 
Asterisk (*), only the numerical value printed on the card 
must be taken into consideration and the Asterisk (*) 
must be ignored.
REVEALED TRAP/PROTECTION CARD (page 17)
Q. When is a Trap Spell considered cast and when is it 
considered revealed?
A. If a Trigger Condition requires a Spell to be Cast, it must 
be read as if the Spell were Revealed. In fact, when a Mage 
performs the Cast a Spell Action on a Trap/Protection 
Spell, it is not revealed, but a Trap/Protection Token 
must be placed on it. From this moment it is no longer 
considered a Ready Spell, but is considered an Active 
Spell. A Trap/Protection Spell is Revealed when the card 
is flipped face up.
ELEMENT, SPELL (page 15)
Q. Does the All Elements  symbol on the Spell Card have 
to be decided when the Spell is revealed? 2) Does the 
choice made last until the end of the current Turn?
A. Whenever a Mage must count the number of their 
Elements to resolve an Effect, they consider the All 
Elements symbol  as 1 symbol of any element of their 
choice. This choice lasts until the Effect is completely 
resolved. Subsequently, when the Mage resolves a 
different Effect in which they must count the number 
of their elements again, they can treat the All Elements 
symbol  as 1 element identical or different from the 
previously chosen one."
AVATAR, EVOCATIONS (page 40)
Q. When an Avatar determines a target Room according to 
the "most Models" criteria, do they also have to consider 
their own Evocations?
A. No, Avatar Evocations must not be considered in this 
case. Even the Avatar model itself does not have to be 
considered when counting the models in the Room 
where the Avatar is located.
FORGOTTEN SPELL OPERA, "IGNORE" 
EFFECT
Q. What Effects are ignored by a Mage who has played the 
Opera Forgotten Spell on themselves?
Can that Mage's Evocations be targeted?
Can that Mage's Instability Cubes be converted?
A. This Spell allows the Mage to ignore the Effects of other 
Mage's Spells. The Spells cast are resolved in their entirety, 
but the Mage targeted by Opera ignores the Effects on 
their Model. Traps and Protections can trigger if Opera's 
target meets the Trigger Condition, but they ignore the 
Effect.
The Opera Effect does not extend to components owned 
by the Mage such as Evocations or Damage/Instability 
Cubes, however if an Effect requires targeting (or 
affecting) the Mage to affect these components, they 
ignore it.
TARGET OF A SPELL
Q. If the text of a Protection Spell says: "You are the Target 
of an  Spell" can I use this protection if the Spell Card 
shows a Room Symbol as the Target and the Effect of that 
Spell applies to my Mage?
A. No, if the Trigger Condition specifies that your Mage 
is the target of a Spell you can resolve the Effect of that 
Protection if the other Mage's Spell targets have one of 
the symbols , , ... or , , ... and if your Mage 
is targeted. 
NIGHTMARE SCHOOL, AVATAR
Q. Can I assign an Umbra to the Avatar?
A. Yes, it is possible to assign an Umbra to an Avatar. During 
the Evocation Phase that Umbra does not inflict Damage 
neither to the Avatar nor to the Evocation on the same 
slot as the Umbra.

NIGHTMARE ACTIVATION
Q. When the Umbra performs a Physical Attack, does it 
target both the Mage and the Evocation that occupies the 
same slot as the Umbra?
Or does it target just one between the Mage and the 
Evocation?
A. When an Umbra performs a Physical Attack the target 
is always the Mage to whom it is assigned. Additionally, 
because of the Effect of the Umbra Evocation Card, its 
controller deals 1 Damage to the Evocation that is in the 
same Slot as the Umbra.
DISCARD SPELL CARDS
Q. When an Effect says to discard a Spell Card, do I always 
have to choose one of the cards from my hand?
A. Yes, in the absence of any other specification the target 
must discard one of the cards from their hand.
CONVERT DAMAGE, SELF
Q. Can I convert other Mage's Damage on my Mage Sheet?
A. No, when a Mage resolves an Effect that allows to convert 
Damage this does not apply to themselves or their 
Evocations.
EFFECTS ON MY MAGE
Q. An Effect inflicts 1 Damage in the target Room and 
moves all models in that Room to an adjacent Room. 
What happens to my Mage if they are in the target Room? 
What happens to one of my Evocation if it is in a target 
Room?
A. According to the Immunity rule, a Mage cannot inflict or 
convert Damage on themselves or their Evocations. Any 
other Effects apply normally.
SOLVE A MISSION
Q. If an Effect allows me to solve a Quest, can I solve the 
Quest's Effect at that time?
A. No. According to the rules, I can resolve the Effect of a 
Completed Quest on my Turn, then that Quest becomes 
Solved and I gain the Power Points. When an Effect 
allows me to Solve a Quest, it means that I immediately 
gain the Power Points indicated by the card, furthermore 
at the end of the game, that Quest counts towards the 
number of Solved Quests. In this case the Quest Effect is 
not resolved.
SPELL CASTING
Q. At what point in the spell casting process (see FAQ 1.1, 
page 3, “Casting a Spell”) is the Mage considered to 
have cast a spell? This is important for certain Traps that 
trigger when a Mage has cast a spell.
A. The process of “Casting a Spell” begins with the action 
of revealing a Spell Card and ends with the complete 
resolution of that Spell's Effect.
“WHEN,” “AFTER,” AND “EACH TIME”
Q. What are the differences between the terms “When,” 
“After,” and “Each time”?
A. The phrases “When,” “After,” and “Each time” can be 
considered synonyms in this game.
ALCHEMY SPELL - FOUNTAIN OF THE THREE
Q. Is Enhancement part of the "OR" Effect, or is it actually 
always applied even if I choose to resolve the phrase 
under "OR"?
A. The Enhancement segment is always performed.
DISCARD SPELL CARDS
Q. According to the rulebook, I can discard a Spell Card 
from my hand during the Study Phase. Where is a 
Personal Spell Card discarded during this phase placed?
A. When a Mage discards a Spell, they place it in their 
Memories. If an Effect indicates that a Spell Card must 
be discarded into the discard pile of its School of Magic 
(Library discard pile), a Mage may discard a Personal 
Spell, but it is removed from the game.
LARGE EVOCATION (page 20)
Q. Can I summon an Evocation with a large base (such as 
the Colossus) and a Forgotten Evocation larger than the 
large Evocation Base?
A. No, an Evocation that is larger than the large Evocation 
Base must be considered as a large Evocation, the same as 
a Colossus.
TRIGGER CONDITION, RESOLVE (page 18)
Q. If multiple Mages have Traps/Protections triggered at the 
same time, in what order should they declare whether to 
resolve the Effects? In what order are they resolved?
A. When a Trigger Condition occurs, each Mage, in Order 
of Play, has a chance to resolve one or more Effects that 
require that Trigger Condition. If a Mage must resolve 
more than one Effect they choose the order in which 
those Effects are resolved. If there are Effects that must 
be resolved by the Black Rose these take precedence 
over any Mage, that is, they must be resolved before the 
Effects of the First Mage. If a Mage must resolve one 
Effect involving multiple targets that Effect is considered 
resolved simultaneously on all targets.
So if a Mage does not resolve any Effect when a Trigger 
Condition has occurred, they cannot choose to do so 
after another Mage has resolved an Effect with the same 
Trigger Condition. After each Mage, in Play Order, has 
resolved their Effects or declared that they will not resolve 
any Effect, the game continues.

JINX AND EVENTS, TRAP/PROTECTIONS, 
RESOLVE (page 18)
Q. In what order are the Events, the Jinxes, and the Traps/
Protections that would be resolved if the same condition 
were to occur?
A. Some game components such as Events, Jinxes, Traps/
Protections or other game components have an Effect that 
must be resolved when a specific condition occurs. For 
what concerns Trap/Protection Spells, the resolution of 
these Effects is explained in detail, and Effects generated 
by other components follow the same rule.
When a condition occurs, the game continues until the 
period (.) of the current Effect; then, in Play Order, each 
Mage has the opportunity to resolve one or more Effects 
triggered by that condition and generated by any game 
component.
If a Mage needs to resolve more than one Effect they 
choose the order in which they are resolved. If an Effect 
must be resolved by the Black Rose, this takes precedence 
over any Mage, it must be resolved before the Effects of 
the First Mage.
If a mage must resolve an Effect involving multiple 
targets that Effect is considered resolved on all targets at 
the same time.
AVATAR, TRAP/PROTECTION (page 38)
Q. Can an Avatar resolve a Command Card with Trap/
Protection symbol if they have already reached the card 
limit for the current Action Phase?
A. No, if the card limit has already been reached the Avatar 
cannot resolve other Trap/Protection Effects.
PLACE / MOVE (page 38)
Q. Is an effect that places a model in a Room considered a 
movement? 
Is the model considered to have entered that Room in 
both cases?
A. Place and Move are different keywords. This applies even 
if the Place keyword resolves in a Room adjacent (or in 
the same Room) to where the model was located.
A model is considered to have entered a Room whether it 
moves or places itself in that Room.
ACTIVE SPELL, MOMENTUM (page 38)
Q. A non revealed Spell with a Trap/Protection token is 
called an Active Spell. 
Is it still considered a Ready Spell?
Can I discard an Active Spell to perform a Momentum 
Action?
A. No, an Active Spell is not considered a Ready Spell, 
therefore it is not possible to discard an Active Spell to 
perform a Momentum Action.
FORGOTTEN EVOCATIONS, SUMMON (page 21)
Q. If an Effect allows me to summon an Evocation of a 
certain archetype, can I choose a Forgotten Evocation 
with that archetype?
A. No, Forgotten Evocations can only be summoned by 
Forgotten Spells or Effects that specify to summon a 
Forgotten Evocation.
ARCHETYPE (page 21)
Q. Are an Evocation and a Forgotten Evocation with the 
same Archetype considered the same Archetype for the 
purposes of Game Effects?
A. Yes, They are the same archetype.
For example, an Evocation Card that says Forgotten 
Beast identifies a (Forgotten) Evocation with the Beast 
Archetype.
DEFEATED MAGE, ONLY ONE COLOR (page 
28)
Q. A Mage is defeated by a text Effect, without being 
inflicted Damage equal to their Health value. If that Mage 
only suffered Damage of one color, does the Mage who 
inflicted that Damage gain 5?
A. The Mage gains 5 when an opposing Mage is defeated 
and that Mage is the only one to have inflicted Damage to 
the defeated Mage.
MAGE DEFEAT (page 28)
Q. If a Mage is defeated while resolving an Effect, after the 
defeat is resolved, that Mage continues resolving the 
interrupted Effect.
What happens if the defeated Mage had more Effects to 
resolve?
For example if they had performed an Explore Action 
with multiple "Move 1" Effects to resolve.
Are Effects not yet resolved lost or can they be resolved?
A. After a Mage's defeat has been resolved that Mage 
continues to resolve only the Effect that was interrupted.
All other Effects produced by the same Action are lost.
This rule is given on page 18 in the section Timing of 
Resolution of Trap/Protection Spells, but it applies in any 
case where a Mage is defeated while resolving an Effect.
AVAILABLE EVOCATION, CLEAN-UP PHASE 
(page 31)
Q. At point 1 of the Cleanup Phase it is stated that the 
Evocations are available again.
Does this mean that the Evocations can be activated 
again in the following Turn or does it mean that they are 
removed from the Lodge and must be returned to the 
Evocation deck?
A. The sentence in this paragraph is not very clear. 
It refers to the case when the optional "Crown of the 
Summoner" rule is used and indicates the time when 
Evocation Cards are rotated to their original position.
Evocations are not removed because of this rule.
EQUIPMENT LIMIT
Q. Is there a limit to the number of Equipment a Mage can 
own?
Is it possible to sum the Effects of two or more Equipment 
that provide a similar bonus or to the same characteristic?
A. The Effects of Equipment with different names are 
added together, however a Mage cannot have 2 or more 
Equipment with the same name.

DISCARD A QUEST
Q. If an Effect indicates a Mage to discard 1 Quest (without 
any other details), can they choose to discard only one of 
their Active Quests or can they also choose one of their 
Completed or Solved Quests?
A. The mage targeted by this Effect must choose one of their 
own Active Quests, Completed Quests or Solved Quests.
EVOCATIONS AVAILABLE,
COMPONENTS AVAILABLE
Q. Which Evocations are defined as available to be 
summoned?
For example, if an Effect allows me to summon an 
Evocation with a Health value of 3 or less, can I choose 
an Evocation that is not summoned by any of the Schools 
of Magic used in the current game?
A. All Evocations in the game are considered available. 
However, before starting a game, players could mutually 
define which components and expansions to use for the 
current game.
This applies to Evocations but also to Upgrade Cards and 
all other game components.
MECHANICAL ARCHIVE, TECHNOMANCY
Q. The Mechanical Archive card from the Technomancy 
school tells you to reveal a Spell Card. 
Is this considered as a Revealed Spell?
A. The Effect of this card means that the chosen card must 
be shown to the other players, then it is drawn by the 
player. 
This is not considered a Revealed Spell.
STEAL, POWER POINTS
Q. Can I steal Power Points from a Mage with 0 Power 
Points?
A. Stealing an amount of Power Points means that the target 
loses that amount of Power Points and the Mage gains 
that same amount of Power Points. If the target has fewer 
Power Points than indicated they lose all the Power Points 
they had, then the Mage gains Power Points equal to the 
amount lost by the target.
Stealing can also be applied to other game elements such 
as Quests or Trophies, the same rule must be followed.
PHYSICAL ATTACK
Q. A Spell allows my Mage to perform a Physical Attack. Is 
this Damage considered inflicted by the Spell?
A. No, in this case the Damage is considered to be inflicted 
by a Physical Attack.
DISCARDED SPELL, PERSISTENT EFFECT
Q. Does a Persistent Effect of a Spell remain active if that 
Spell is discarded from the Mage Sheet? For example, if it 
is moved to the Memories via the Armory Room.
A. A Persistent Effect is no longer active if the Spell that 
generated it is no longer in the corresponding Space of 
the Mage Sheet. In this case the Persistent Token returns 
to the Mage's personal reserve.
SPELL DISCARDED, EFFECT INTERRUPTED
Q. If a Spell is discarded from the Mage Sheet while its Effect 
is being resolved, is the Effect interrupted or resolved 
completely?
A. In this case the Spell Effect is resolved up to the first 
period (.) then the Card is discarded. The rest of the 
Effect is ignored.
COLOSSUS, DAMAGE
Q. If a Colossus Evocation and a Mage are moved into 
a Room due to an Effect, does that Colossus inflict 3 
Damage to that Mage?
A. No, because the Effect of the Colossus Evocation Card 
applies to a Mage who enters a Room where the Colossus 
is already present.
QUEST, MASTER OF DEATH
Q. If a Mage is in their Cell can they be chosen for the Effect 
of the "Master of Death" Quest?
(Rebirth Core Box, Third Moon Quest)
A. No, a Mage in their Cell cannot be chosen by an Effect, 
therefore not even by the Effect of this Quest.
CELL
Q. When a Mage is in the Cell, that Mage cannot be targeted 
or influenced in any way. Does this refer only to the Mage's 
Model or to all components and game values (e.g. Power 
Points) of that Mage?
For example, is it possible to target one of their Evocations 
or one Instability Cube? Can that Mage lose Power Points?
A. An Effect that removes 1 Instability from a target Room 
can be applied even if the Mage owner of that Instability is 
in the Cell. The same applies to any other Effect that affects 
a component owned by that Mage and does not require 
targeting the owning Mage.
However, if, for example, an Effect requires you to choose a 
target Mage, you cannot choose a Mage who is in the Cell.
A Mage in the Cell plays normally with the following 
exceptions:
• During their Activation (or at the start of their 
Activation), a Mage who is in a Cell must perform a 
Physical Action or a Momentum Action to leave the 
Cell. They can then choose to resolve other Effects. As 
long as they are in the Cell, all phrases in their Physical/
Momentum Action that do not allow them to leave the 
Cell are ignored.
• A Mage in the Cell cannot be targeted for Effects resolved 
by other Mages. A Mage in the Cell ignores any Effects 
(in any part of it) resolved by other Mages, except Effects 
that specify that they apply to a Mage in the Cell.
• The Effects of Event Cards also apply to Mages in the 
Cell.
The Cell cannot be chosen as a target for Effects that 
require a , , , ,  target. The Cell ignores all 
Effects that would apply to a Room, with the exception of 
Effects that specify that they apply to a Cell.

CELL
Q. Can a Mage who is in the Cell be placed in a different 
Room by resolving the Final Duel Trap Effect?
A. Yes, when this spell is resolved, the Mage in the Cell may 
leave the Cell.
COMPATIBILITY
Q. Some Effects in Black Rose Wars have phrases containing 
multiple keywords such as ‘inflict’ or ‘summon’. How 
should these Effects be resolved with the new Traps/
Protections rules?
A. In order to resolve Traps and Protections these Effects 
should be understood as if the different keywords were 
written in several sentences separated by a period (.).
DEFEATED, REMOVED
Q. What is the difference between a defeated or removed 
model? There are Effects that refer to only one of these 
terms.
A. A Mage or Evocation is defeated when it takes a number 
of Damage equal to its Health value. When the defeat of 
an Evocation is resolved, its model is removed from the 
Lodge and its Evocation Card is returned to the deck of 
available Evocations. Some Effects allow you to remove 
an Evocation without it being defeated, i.e. regardless of 
how much Damage it has suffered. When an Evocation is 
removed due to an Effect it is not considered defeated.
PLACE INSTABILITY
Q. A condition requires me to place 2 Instabilities, if I play 
an Effect that places 2 or more Instabilities but the target 
Room has only 1 free slot, is the condition considered as 
fulfilled?
A. No, the condition is not considered to be fulfilled.
INFLICT DAMAGE (page 28)
Q. Can I inflict more Damage to a Model than its Health 
value?
A. A Model can suffer a maximum amount of Damage equal 
to its own Health value. When an Effect inflicts Damage 
on a Model it can inflict a maximum amount of Damage 
equal to the target's Health value minus any Damage that 
target has already suffered before the Effect is resolved. 
The amount of Damage inflicted on a target is always 
equal to the amount of Damage suffered from that Effect.
VULNERABILITY, JINX
Q. Can a Mage inflict 1 Damage to the target Mage when the 
Jinx card Vulnerability is assigned to the target?
A. No. Vulnerability allows the Mage to inflict 1 Damage 
on the target Mage each time a Jinx is assigned if 
Vulnerability is already assigned to the target.
JINX SPELL - SUFFOCATION
Q. My Mage has the Weakness Jinx assigned, and another 
Mage has the Suffocation Trap ready. In my Activation, I 
activate a Green Room that allows me to place the third 
Cube on the Jinx. The Jinx is discarded, but the Trap can 
only target a Mage with a Jinx. In what order are these 
Effects resolved?
A. When a Condition occurs (in this case, Activating a 
Room), each Mage in Play Order may resolve one or 
more Effects that require that Condition. So once the 
Room is Activated, the First Mage may choose to resolve 
an Effect, and so on. If the Mage who activated the Room 
is also the First Mage, they can discard the Jinx and the 
other Mage cannot resolve their Trap.
AVATAR COMMAND CARD (page 36)
Q. When an Avatar Command Card is resolved, does it count 
both as a Revealed Spell and as a Physical Action for the 
purposes of trigger conditions of other components?
A. A single Command Card can contain several different 
Effects, it can contain both a Physical Action and a Spell.
When a Command Card is revealed the Avatar is 
considered to have revealed a Spell Card if that Command 
Card has a Spell Effect. When a Physical Action Effect 
is resolved the Avatar is considered to have performed a 
Physical Action.
CUBES ON QUEST AND JINX (page 12)
Q. When I complete a Jinx/Quest, do the Cubes return to 
the Mage's Pool?
A. Yes, the cubes return to the Mage's Pool.
LIQUID FIRE SPELL, SCHOOL OF ALCHEMY
Q. The effect of Spell Liquid Fire (Dark Side) targets a Room. 
Does the Enhancement part apply to my construct in the 
targeted Room or to any of my constructs in the Lodge?
A. The Card Effect applies in full to the Target Room unless 
otherwise stated. In this case the construct must be in the 
Target Room.

EFFECT "EACH TIME"
Q. Some Effects have the condition “each time” occurring in 
the text. How can you distinguish something that occurs 
at the same time from something that occurs at two 
separate times?
For example, the Forgotten Spell Cursed Bond: “Each 
time the target places Instability, you place the same 
amount of Instability in a . ”
If I activate the Garden Room (Rebuilt) Effect, can I apply 
the Cursed Bond Effect only once or twice?
Garden Room (Rebuilt) : “ Place 1 Instability. 
Place 1 Instability. “ .
A. Two sentences separated by a period (.) are considered 
different moments.
The condition required by an Effect (in the same way 
as the Trigger Condition of Traps and Protections) is 
checked at the end of each period (.) of the Effect being 
resolved.
In the case where the Garden Room (Rebuilt) is triggered, 
the Cursed Bond Effect is applied twice.
Instead, an Effect: “Place 1 Instability in each .” is to be 
considered resolved at a single time.
CA' DARIO'S AMULET SPELL, SCHOOL OF 
HEX
Q. Does the Ca' Dario's Amulet Spell Card also deal 2 
Damage to a target Mage who has no Jinx assigned?
A. Yes, the number of Jinxes assigned only affects the 
previous sentence concerning stealing PP. The Spell deals 
2 Damage regardless of the Jinxes assigned to the target.
EFFECTS "OR"
Q. Some Effects contain the word "or", how should they be 
read?
For example, if I reveal the Slicing Spell from the War 
School, can I move 1 and then choose one of the two 
parts or can I only move if I choose the first part?
A. The word "or" between two sentences is meant to divide 
the text into two parts. The Mage chooses whether to 
resolve the part before “or” or the part after “or.”
TROPHY
Q. Some Effects allow you to discard, gain, take or steal a 
Trophy Token. 
1.What is the difference between these words?
2.Can I discard 1 Trophy of my color to perform an

DESTINY TOKEN AND INFECTIO TOKEN
Q. Are Destiny or Infectio Tokens resolved even if the Mage 
they are assigned to is placed in their Cell?
A. Yes, they are resolved even if the Mage is placed in their Cell.
DESTINY TOKEN AND INFECTIO TOKEN
Q. Some Effects are resolved at step 2 of the Cleanup Phase. 
In what order are they resolved if more than one such 
Effects are resolved in the same phase?
A. At step 2 of the Cleanup Phase, Effects are resolved in Play 
Order. If a Mage has more than one Effect to resolve, they 
choose in which order to resolve the Effects. All Infectio 
Tokens from the same Mage assigned to multiple targets 
are considered resolved at the same time. 
All Destiny Tokens from the same Mage assigned to 
multiple targets are considered resolved at the same time. 
The Mage chooses in which order to resolve the different 
types of tokens.
HORDE FEAT
Q. The Collective Sacrifice Quest (Third Moon) requires you 
to Heal Damage to Evocations. Is the Horde Talent valid 
for the purpose of this Quest Task? How many times is 
the Task fulfilled?
A. The Quest Task is considered fulfilled once when in the 
Cleanup Phase the Mage heals all Damage on one or 
more of their own Evocations.
HEAL INFECTIO OR DESTINY
Q. Is it possible to Heal an Infectio Token or a Destiny 
Token?
A. Unless otherwise specified, “Heal” applies only to 
Damage, so it is not possible to Heal an Infectio or 
Destiny Token.

PESTIS ACTIVATION
Q. In the Evocations Phase, how do I keep track of which 
Pestis I have already activated? Do they activate one at a 
time or all at once?
A. The Mage chooses in what order to activate each of their

component to mark which Pestis has already activated 
and which have not yet, however it is useful to remember
