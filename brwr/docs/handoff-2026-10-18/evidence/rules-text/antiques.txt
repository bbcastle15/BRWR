CACUS SETUP
Apply the following steps during step 3 of the Avatar Setup:
• The First Mage places the Cacus Model in a Room adjacent to their Cell.
• Remove the Titan's Attack card from the Forgotten Spells Deck. 
• Place the Cacus Avatar Card (not the Enraged Cacus one) on the Avatar Card Slot 
of the Avatar Board.
• Place the Enraged Cacus Avatar Card on one side of the table.
Avata r — C ac u s
