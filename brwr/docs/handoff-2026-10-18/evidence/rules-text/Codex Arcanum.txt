Ten years of chasing nothing more than a whisper. Ten years of wandering driven by the wind. Ten years of 
searching for something that long ago ceased to exist.
Ten years ago, Alessandro first heard the Voice of the Black Rose. Thorny twigs led him through the depths of 
great cities and the thickets of the darkest forests in search of a book hidden from the eyes of the ignorant.
Each library sealed shut and each of the temples forgotten for centuries was a chance to end his journey, but each 
of them turned out to be just another stop on a never-ending road. Until now. 
A single note written in faded ink mentions the name "Codex Arcanum". The thorned vines grow anew, 
marking the next destination on his path.
Ten years of chasing nothing more than a whisper. Ten years of wandering driven by the wind. Ten years of 
searching for something that might still exist.

USING THE CODEX
Codex is divided into twenty-seven chapters , which are to be consulted as indicated below in order to understand the 
game as simply and straightforwardly as possible.
1. Schools of Magic: these chapters present all the Schools of Magic available in the game. Clicking on the elements 
contained in the Y - Related Mages sections will take you to the corresponding entries in the Codex.
2. Mages: this chapter presents all the Mages available in the game, along with their unique components. Clicking 
on the elements in the Suggested School of Magic section will take you to the corresponding chapter of the 
Codex.
3. The Lodge: this chapter presents all the rooms available in the game.
4. Lodge Layouts: this chapter describes how to prepare the Lodge for a game with a specific number of players.
5. Evocations: this chapter presents all the Evocations available in the game.
6. Upgrade Cards: this chapter presents all the Upgrade Cards available in the game.
7. Jinx Cards: this chapter presents all the Jinx Cards available in the game.
8. Marks of the Old Ones: this chapter presents all the Marks of the Old Ones Cards available in the game and the 
rules that let you use them in Black Rose Wars games.
9. Errata: this chapter is a collection of patches released for the game. Other sections of the Codex contain references 
to this chapter, marked with the symbol 3 or subsections 3 - Errata. Clicking on the symbol 3 or on one of 
the subsection elements will take you to the corresponding patch.

TRANSFORMATION
Some Spell Cards allow you to perform a Transformation as part of one of their Effects. A Transformation is identified by the 
Transform keyword followed by the name of the Transformation. When a Mage performs Transform, they follow these steps:
• They replace their Mage's Model with the Model of the indicated Transformation, if available; otherwise, they ignore 
the Transformation.
• They discard all their Equipment Evocations.
• They place the ; that transformed them next to their Mage Sheet, rotated as indicated by the Transform Effect.
As long as that ; remains next to their Mage Sheet, the Mage is considered Transformed and the Physical Actions listed on that 
; are available to that Mage. When it is in that position, that ; is considered the ; that Transformed the Mage.
The Mage discards the ; that transformed them when:
• They are defeated.
• They are about to perform Transform.
When this happens, that Mage is no longer Transformed and must replace the Model of their Transformation with the Model of 
their Mage.
At the Beginning of the Evocations Phase, in play order, a Transformed Mage may perform one of the Physical Actions listed on
the ; that transformed them without exhausting a <.
SPIRITUAL PROPAGATION 
Some Spells of this School have Effects with the Bjorn or Loki keyword. When a Mage resolves a Spell of this School, they apply 
the Effect phrases after these keywords only if they are Transformed into the corresponding Transformation. 
FERAL MAGIC 
At Step 1 of the first Study Phase of the game, instead of drawing 2; from their Grimoire, each Mage may search their Grimoire 
or Memories for 1; of this School that contains the Transformed keyword. If they do, they draw that card and shuffle the 
Grimoire, then they draw 1; from their Grimoire. If they do not, they perform the Study Phase normally. 
COMMUNION WITH NATURE
