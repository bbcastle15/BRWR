Familiars
A Familiar is a special Evocation that a Mage can summon using the Room Effect associated with it. 
The Familiar has a special bond with the Mage who summoned it, serving them with its powers and 
protecting them at the same time.
To use Familiars in a Black Rose Wars game, follow the directions below.
Setup
Place the Familiar Room in the Lodge, replacing a Room of the same color chosen by 
common agreement among the players. Place all other Familiar materials to form a reserve 
accessible to all players.

Summoning a Familiar 
A Familiar can be summoned only if one of its Models is available in the reserve. When a 
Mage summons a Familiar, they place the Model of that Familiar in the Room they are in, 
attached to their own Model (Guard Position). They then take the Familiar Card and place 
it in one of their own available Evocation Slots, and then place the Familiar Action Token 
on the Familiar Action Token Slot, showing the active side.
A Mage can have a maximum of one Familiar among their Evocations.
Available side
Exhausted side
Familiar’s Actions
The Mage who owns a Familiar has access to 
new Actions, which can be used by flipping 
the Familiar Action Token on its Exhausted 
side. These Actions are:
• Assault: only if the Familiar is in Guard 
Position, the Familiar switches to Hunt 
Position and its Model releases from that 
of the owning Mage, then the Familiar 
activates. 
• Flanking: only if the Familiar is in Hunt 
Position and is in the same Room as the 
Model of the owning Mage, the Familiar 
switches to Guard Position (the two Models 
attach).
• Concentration: only if the Familiar is 
in Guard Position, apply the Effect of the 
Familiar’s Guarding Skill.
An Exhausted Familiar Action Token 
becomes Available again at the end of step 1
of the next Clean-up Phase.

Familiar’s Positions
A Familiar is always in one Position of the two available; these are:
 Guard Position: in this Position the Model of the Familiar and the Model of the 
owning Mage are attached and are considered as one Model.
 Hunt Position: in this Position the Model of the Familiar and the Model of the 
owning Mage are released, and are considered two separate Models. 
Each Familiar’s Position has specific rules: 
GUARD POSITION RULES 
In this position, these rules apply to the Familiar:
• It is not considered in the Lodge.
• Its Hunting Skill is not active.
• It can sustain an amount of  instead of its owning Mage, up to the number of 
free Health Slots on its Familiar Card. The owning Mage can freely choose how to 
distribute this .
• The owning Mage can use the Concentration Action.
• The owning Mage can use the Assault Action.
• When the owning Mage is defeated, if the Familiar doesn’t have all its Health Slots 
occupied by , the Familiar remains in the Room in Hunt Position.
HUNT POSITION RULES 
In this position, these rules apply to the Familiar:
• Is considered an Evocation of the owning Mage.
• Its Hunting Skill is active.
• If at the end of the Clean-up Phase the Familiar is within a  from the owning Mage 
Model, it can move to a Guard Position without spending the Familiar Action 
Token.
