Schools of Magic
Schools of Magic are fully compatible between the two games. 
To include Schools of Magic from both games to the game, 
at step 9 of chapter 1 - Setup (page 8), form the Library by 
including 6 Schools of Magic chosen randomly or by mutual 
agreement by the players from all those available, whether they 
come from Black Rose Wars or Black Rose Wars: Rebirth.
ANATOMY DIFFERENCE
Spell Cards in Black Rose Wars are anatomically diff erent 
from those in Black Rose Wars: Rebirth in several ways. 
Since it is necessary to refer to the rulebook included in 
Black Rose Wars: Rebirth, these anatomical diff erences are 
highlighted here, along with how each should be considered 
in order to work with the newer rulebook.
LIGHT SIDE AND DARK SIDE
Spell Cards in Black Rose Wars do not have a distinction 
between Light Side and Dark Side like the ones in Black 
Rose Wars: Rebirth. They used to show one side called 
the Straight Side, recognizable by the arrow in the upper left 
corner of the card A , and one side called the Reverse Side. 
For any indication of the rules and materials, the straight side 
of Black Rose Wars Spell Cards is equivalent to the Light Side, 
while the reverse side is equivalent to the Dark Side.

Evocations
Evocation Cards (and Evocations) are fully compatible 
between the two games. To include Evocations from both 
games in a game, create a Evocation Card deck by shuffl ing the 
Evocation Cards from Black Rose Wars with those from Black 
Rose Wars: Rebirth.
Add the Evocation Models from both games to the game pool.
Rose Pins and Evocation Bases
Black Rose Wars Models have up to three slots on their bases, 
into which the owner inserts one or more of their Rose Pins

Large Models
In Black Rose Wars: Rebirth each Mage has at their disposal a 
Large Evocation Base that features no  symbol.
As stated on chapter 2 - Game Turn in the rulebook, Summon 
Evocations paragraph (page 20), a Mage may only own one 
Model clipped to the Large Evocation Base. This rule applies 
only to Models featured in Black Rose Wars: Rebirth and 
Models featured in later releases; put more simply, it means all 
Evocation Models that do not have slots in their bases.
Evocation Models that have slots in their bases follow the 
normal rules explained in the previous paragraph, without some 
limitations based on the size of their base. Some Black Rose 
Wars Evocations can house a single Rose Pin; this indicates that 
a Mage can own a single copy of such Evocations. Even if a 
single Rose Pin is placed on the base of the Model, the Evocation 
Card of these Models can be placed in any of the Evocation Slots 
(, ,   ) of the Mage Sheet, chosen at the time the 
Model is summoned.
REMOVE EVOCATIONS
Some game Eff ects allow you to remove an Evocation from the Lodge. When a Mage resolves one of these Eff ects they 
must comply with these points:
• A non-Forgotten Spell cannot remove another Mages's Forgotten Evocation.
• A non-Forgotten Spell cannot remove another Mage's Evocation with a Health value greater than 3. Note that some 
Eff ects may change the Health value of an Evocation from that printed on the Evocation Card.
• An Eff ect that removes your own Evocation allows the Mage to ignore the previous points; therefore you can remove 
your Evocation with any Health value even if it is a Forgotten Evocation.
• A Forgotten Spell that removes an Evocation ignores the previous points; therefore you can remove one of your own 
(or another Mages's) Evocation with any Health value even if it is a Forgotten Evocation.

Rooms
To use the Rooms in Black Rose Wars and Black Rose Wars: 
Rebirth, before performing the game setup, choose randomly 
or by mutual agreement 19 Rooms with which to form the 
Lodge, making sure that each of the Rooms listed below is 
chosen in single copy, regardless of the game they come from.
The Rooms from Black Rose Wars start the game with their 
Front facing up and the corresponding Activation Token on 
them. 
Rooms from Black Rose Wars: Rebirth start the game with 
the Destroyed side facing up. 
Each Room used in the game will then follow the rules given in 
the rulebook of its respective game.

Sator Box Rooms
To include Rooms from the Sator Box in a game, perform 
game setup until you have a defi ned Lodge layout. Then select 
the Rooms from the Sator Box that you want to include. For 
each Room from the Sator Box selected, randomly choose a 
Room from the Lodge that has the same color and swap them, 
removing the Room just taken out of the Lodge from the game.
If, during the game, a player draws a Quest Card that requires 
to be resolved in a Room that is not in the Lodge, they may 
publicly display the Quest Card, discard it, and draw another 
one. 
In the event that Rooms from both Black Rose Wars and 
Black Rose Wars: Rebirth are used in the same game, 
when calculating the End of the Game points on page 32, 
assign the Mages who have one or more Activation Tokens 
from a Room from Black Rose Wars the  Power Points 
associated with each of those Tokens.
