Familiars
A Familiar is a special Evocation that a Mage can summon using the Room Eff ect associated with it. 
The Familiar has a special bond with the Mage who summoned it, serving them with its powers and 
protecting them at the same time.
To use Familiars in a Black Rose Wars game, follow the directions below.
Setup
Place the Familiar Room in the Lodge, replacing a Room of the same color chosen by 
common agreement among the players. Place all other Familiar materials to form a reserve 
accessible to all players.
Summoning a Familiar 
A Familiar can be summoned only if one of its Models is available in the reserve. When a 
Mage summons a Familiar, they place the Model of that Familiar in the Room they are in, 
attached to their own Model (Guard Position). They then take the Familiar Card and place 
it in one of their own available Evocation Slots, and then place the Familiar Action Token 
on the Familiar Action Token Slot, showing the active side.
A Mage can have a maximum of one Familiar among their Evocations.

Familiar’s Actions
The Mage who owns a Familiar has access to 
new Actions, which can be used by fl ipping 
the Familiar Action Token on its Exhausted 
side. These Actions are:
• Assault: only if the Familiar is in Guard 
Position, the Familiar switches to Hunt 
Position and its Model releases from that 
of the owning Mage, then the Familiar 
activates. 
• Flanking: only if the Familiar is in 
Hunt Position and is in the same Room 
as the Model of the owning Mage, the 
Familiar switches to Guard Position (the 
two Models attach).
• Concentration: only if the Familiar is in 
Guard Position, apply the Eff ect of the 
Familiar’s Guarding Skill.
An Exhausted Familiar Action Token 
becomes Available again at the end of step 1
of the next Clean-up Phase
