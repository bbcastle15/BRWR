## Resumed work

Umbra are independent 120-degree annular tokens attached to the assigned Mage.
Three segments close the circle; the Mage centre stays clickable and the
player number is below the ring. Each sector has its own target/inspect
hitbox. Nightmare assignment offers the three unoccupied slots.

31 additional Mage definitions and Personal Spells have been sourced from
Codex pp. 68–82; **30 are selectable**. Shared effects cover the new cards.
Personal Spells remain excluded from School decks. Original Codex card
extracts in `assets/spells/personal_codex` are fallback artwork for these and
15 older Mages; user-supplied full-resolution card art takes precedence.

Agorix's Familiar Eracleto uses the existing Evocation collection, with Guard
and Hunt, an independent Familiar Action token, damage distribution, the Hunt
attack bonus, defeat handling and the optional cleanup return to Guard.
Its card is an unmodified embedded Codex image. Doll has both Undead and
Construct archetypes and its optional effect before removal on defeat.

Additional corrections verified while implementing these effects:

- Jotuns Vindar reacts after the leaving Model's position is committed and
  before entering-room effects; an interrupted move is not resumed blindly.
- Alice's Light Spell bonus and Instability use the damaged Mage's Room.
- Drums of War shares its maximum 3 Power reward across the repeated attacks.
- Self-targeted Power loss applies to the caster; Irene's Forgotten draw
  reaction also works when another Mage draws a Forgotten Spell.
- Cosma and Arianna use Evocation targets and the appropriate filters.

**Hyperion is not complete and remains unavailable** (`implemented:false`).
The remaining work is its three Mage Models, shared damage, three Cells,
profile changes, model choice for Effects/Actions, Phalanx and Personal Spell.
Do not enable it just because its art and JSON are present. The BRWR rules are
the final pages of `../materials/NAR_Hyperion_Rulebook_ENG.pdf`; this is not the
Forgotten Evocation version of Hyperion.

12 Hex Light review variants are in `assets/spells/hex_light_review`.
`gallery.html` compares each with its original; `manifest.json` records image
generation provenance. They are review copies only, excluded from runtime.
Original PNGs were not overwritten; generated variants need visual approval
before adoption, particularly for fine printed details.

## Browser delivery

The optimized Web exporter uses a temporary copy with texture size 1024 and
quality 0.67; original assets are preserved. The multiplayer compatibility
fingerprint now includes `mage_effects.gd` and `familiar_rules.gd`.

Final source/export parity and bundle hashes are recorded in
`evidence/nov03-export-baseline.json` and `evidence/nov03-web-bundle.json`
under `docs/handoff-2026-10-18`. Both the source and exported pack must expose
Nightmare and the 30 additional Mages, load the same available artwork and
produce the same multiplayer version.

Final parity passed: **239 Spell textures, 57 Mage cards, 45 identical source
hashes and the same multiplayer compatibility version**. The host launcher's
`tools/check_web_export.gd` also passed. The original missing artwork for
unfinished School content is recorded separately in the baseline.

Updated `output/web` and `deploy/web_bundle`: PCK **97.99 MiB**, gzip **95.30
MiB**. They use five and four parts respectively, at most 24 MiB per part.
The SHA-256 of each concatenated bundle matches its exported file; all other
Web runtime files were copied and hash checked.

No commit, push or public deployment has been performed. The hosted site still
needs the updated bundle published.
