Some Spells of this School assign Jinx Cards to a target Mage. When assigned, a Jinx card has to be taken from its deck and placed 
next to the target's Mage Sheet. Each Jinx Card imposes a negative Effect on the Mage it is assigned to. Whenever a Mage fulfills 
the Task requirement of a Jinx assigned to them, they place a * on the leftmost Slot of that Jinx Card. When a Mage has placed a 
* on each Cube Slot of a Jinx he assigned to them, they discard that Jinx.  
A Mage can have any number of Jinx Cards assigned to them, but no more than 1 Jinx Card with the same name can be 
assigned to the same Mage. If an Effect would assign to a Mage a second Jinx with the same name, that Jinx is not assigned.  
If an Effect assigns a specific Jinx but there are no more cards of that Jinx left in the deck, that Jinx is not assigned.
