HOW TO READ THIS RULEBOOK
This rulebook is divided into eight chapters, which are to be consulted as indicated below to understand the game in the simplest 
and most straightforward way possible.
1. Components: All the game components included in the box are displayed visually in this chapter.
2. Basic Concepts: This chapter introduces the game without going into detail about the rules, instead it provides a basis for 
a better understanding of the following chapters.
3. Setup: This chapter contains all the instructions for setting up the playing area.
4. Game Turn: This chapter explains in detail the rules to follow during the fi ve phases that make up a Game Turn. It clarifi es 
the fl ow of actions that each player must perform, but without going into rules that are not strictly related to a single Game 
Phase. Instead, these are all mentioned with cross-references and indications pointing to the chapter that covers each one.
5. End of game Tally: This chapter lists the rules for declaring the winner at the end of the game.
6. Rules Compendium: This chapter explains in detail all the in-depth rules of the game or that require the temporary 
interruption of the game fl ow in order to be applied.
7. Duel Avatar: This chapter explains how to insert the Avatar into a game, a third player guided by the Black Rose and 
controlled by an A.I.
8. Beyond the Duel: This chapter provides practical information on how to use the contents of this box with the other 
products in the Black Rose Wars line.
Hidden among the sacred mists of a sanctuary in the hills of Edo, the Lodge of the Sun preserves the ancient 
mystical art of Shugendō. Its monks, led by the legendary En no Gyōja, weave together prayers and spells, 
summoning the power of the Kami, the protective spirits of the people of the East. For centuries, they have watched 
over the fragile balance between the world of the living and that of the spirits, holding back the dark forces that 
threaten their land.
But now a shadow is cast over the Lodge. Abe no Seimei, the most brilliant of the disciples, harbors a forbidden 
ambition—to lead the mages into a new era, where arcane power is no longer used to protect, but to dominate. 
His plots, woven in the shadows to undermine his master, have awakened a forgotten horror—a Mononoke, a 
vengeful spirit thirsty for magic and blood.
Now, the Lodge of the Sun must choose—appease the wrath of the Specter, or perish under the weight of its own 
destiny.

Basic Concepts
Black Rose Wars Duel: Kami is a game of duels between Mages taking 
place in the Lodge of the Sun, a sentient place where a powerful 
supernatural entity, the Black Rose, dwells. 
This is the place where the Mages of the land of Edo gather to learn the 
arcane arts and challenge each other to improve their skills and make 
contact with the benevolent spirits called Kami.
This game allows two players to challenge each other in a fearsome 
magical battle, teaching the fundamentals of its sister game: Black Rose 
Wars. 
This is a stand-alone game, but most of the material in this box can be 
used to expand Black Rose Wars as well. The reverse, however, is not 
always possible (see page 27).

TARGETS
The Eff ects of Spells and other game 
components always have a specifi c target that 
must be chosen from among those available 
by whoever resolves the Eff ect. 
The target of an Eff ect is always indicated 
before the text of the Eff ect itself, followed 
by a colon (:) H , except for the Targets 
of Spell Card Eff ects, which are shown in the 
third banner below the Spell name I .
 
Targets can be:
•  Model: The eff ect can target a Model other than the Model 
that resolves the Eff ect. The number next to the icon (, , ) 
indicates the range, i.e., the maximum number of rooms that the 
target of the Eff ect can be away from the Model that resolves the 
Eff ect. To choose a target it must be possible to draw a straight 
line between the Room the target is in and the Room the Model 
that resolves the Eff ect is in, without the line being blocked by 
Room walls. If the icon is followed by an asterisk (), the eff ect 
may target a model anywhere in the Lodge regardless of distance, 
and even if it's not possible to draw a straight uninterrupted line 
as indicated above.
•  Mage Model: As for  Model, however, only the Model of a 
Mage other than themselves can be targeted.
•  Evocation Model: As for  Model, however, only the Model 
of an Evocation can be targeted.
•  Room: The Eff ect targets a Room and aff ects all opposing 
Models (Mages and their Evocations) inside that Room, or the 
Room itself. The number inside the icon (, , ) indicates 
the range, i.e., the maximum number of rooms that the target 
Room of the Eff ect can be away from the Model that resolves the 
Eff ect. To choose the target Room, it must be possible to draw 
a straight line between the target Room and the Room where the 
Model that resolves the Eff ect is located, without this line being 
blocked by Room walls. If the icon contains an asterisk (), 
the Eff ect may target a Room anywhere in the Lodge regardless 
of the distance and even if it's not possible to draw a straight 
uninterrupted line as indicated above.
•  Personal: These Eff ects target the mage who resolves them.
•  Special: This Eff ect has no target. The Eff ect's text indicates 
how to apply it and which game elements are infl uenced by it.
For examples and in-depth rules on Targets, Range and Line 
of Sight see page 21.

Setup
Follow the steps below to set up the game:
1 Open the game board A and place it in the center of the table.
2 Place the Spiritual Black Rose Room B in the center of 
the board with the Intact side face up, i.e. the side showing the 
Instability Slots (the holes).
 Shuffl e the six remaining Rooms and place them randomly 
around the Spiritual Black Rose Room according to the board 
layout and with their Intact side face up. 
3 Place the Events Card C on the corresponding space on the 
board, so that one of the two sides is visible, chosen randomly or by 
mutual agreement. For each of the two Quests Cards D , choose 
which side will be turned face up by mutual agreement, or choose 
randomly. Then place them in the corresponding spaces on the 
board.
4 Place the Room Activation Tokens E on each Room with 
the same name, with the Available side face up. Place the Black 
Rose Damage/Instability Cubes (black cubes) F next to the 
Game Board, so that they are within easy reach of both players.
5 The player who last received a rose (or a randomly selected 
player) obtains the Crown Token () G , becoming the 
Crown Holder.
6 Starting with the Crown Holder, each player chooses a color 
and sits on the side of the board that corresponds to that color. 
Then they take the Damage/Instability Cubes, the Physical 
Action Tokens, the Persistence Tokens and the Power 
Token corresponding to that color H . 
 The player then chooses a Mage, takes the corresponding Model 
and places it in the Cell I of their color. They take their Mage 
Card and place it on the corresponding space on their side of the 
board and place their Physical Action Tokens J on it with the 
Available side face up. They then take their three Personal Spell 
Cards belonging to their Mage K and keep them face up in 
front of themselves.
7 Take the Shugendō School of Magic deck and place it next to 
the game board so that it is within reach of all players. Place the 
School Reference Card L under the School of Magic deck as 
shown in the image. This deck is referred to as the Library M .
 Shuffl e the available Forgotten Spells and form a deck, place it 
next to the game board in the space provided N .
Place the deck of Evocation Cards within reach O .
8 The Crown Holder takes the School Reference Card
L from the Library and selects one of the two Starting
 Grimoires E listed on it. The player looks through the School 
of Magic deck and takes a copy of each of the six cards listed, 
then adds one of their Mage's Personal Spell Cards to them 
and shuffl es them together. This deck of seven cards is their 
Grimoire; they place it in the space provided on their side of the
 board () P , then takes their other two Personal Spells and 
places them face up in the Memories slot () on their side 
of the game board Q . The other player does the same, but 
chooses the Starting Grimoire that the Crown Holder didn't 
choose.
 Once this process is fi nished, shuffl e the remaining School of 
Magic cards in the Library and place the deck back on the side 
of the board above the School Reference Card. 
9 Each player places one of their Damage/Instability Cubes on 
space 0 of the Trophy Track () and one on space 0 of the 
Rooms Track () on their side of the game board R ; then 
they place their Power Token on space 0 of the Power Track S .
 The Crown Holder places a Black Rose Damage/Instability 
Cube on space 0 of the Trophy Track, the Rooms Track T
and the Turn Track of the Black Rose U ; then they place the 
Black Rose Power Token on space 0 of the Power Track V .
10 Place the two Portals of the same color on the entrances 
indicated in the image W . If both players agree, place the other 
two Portals on the entrances indicated in the image X to have a 
Lodge with more connected Rooms.
Now the game can begin!

The numerical value of the targets is called range, and indicates the 
distance in rooms within which the target chosen for the Eff ect 
can be located. Ranges with a value greater than 0 (   ) 
require line of sight between the caster and the target.
A range  ( ) or with a value of 0 ( ) does not require 
any line of sight.
L I N E O F S I G H T 
The line of sight is an imaginary straight line between the center 
of the Room where the Model resolving the Eff ect is located and 
the center of the Room where the target of the Eff ect or the target 
Room of the Eff ect is located. This line must always run through 
the centers of a row of Rooms, and can never run along the edge 
of a Room. The line goes through a maximum number of Rooms 
(excluding the starting Room) equal to the range indicated on the 
Eff ect target.

D u e l Avata r
The Avatar is the embodiment of the Black Rose's will in a physical 
body. It roams the Lodge carrying out the Rose's schemes and acting 
as her hand, granting power to those she deems worthy and punishing 
those who oppose her will.
Over time, many entities have served as Avatars for the Black Rose—
mechanical knights born of alchemical powers, divine entities, or 
beings from the most remote lands. Even spirits from the world's most 
ancient legends have been servants of the Black Rose, and when she 
deems it necessary, she can summon them to her service.
In the Lodge of the Sun, a terrible Yokai, a nefarious spirit, has been 
chosen as an Avatar...
The Avatar is an Artifi cial Intelligence (AI) that can be used in any 
Black Rose Wars: Duel game, replacing a human player in a solo 
game or joining a two-player game as a third participant, making 
the Black Rose an even more challenging opponent.
The Avatar behaves similarly to a Mage, following most of the 
standard rules.
Mononoke
Mononoke is the Avatar provided in this box. This vengeful spirit 
has infi ltrated the Lodge of the Sun to assassinate and devour the 
magical power of unworthy Mages who dare to challenge it.
Mononoke has three Skills described on the back of its Avatar Card.
Read the Skills carefully before starting the game, as each one must 
be applied each time it is possible to do so.
