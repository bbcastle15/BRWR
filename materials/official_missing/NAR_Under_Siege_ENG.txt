Several years have passed since your group’s last adventure. Rome 
has welcomed all of you back, and life goes on peacefully within 
her walls. Each of you has taken a diff erent path, but every now 
and then you manage to get together for a drink and recall the 
good old days. Th e amber color of the beer, sipped as you bask 
in front of a large fi replace, seems to come alive as the irregular 
glass of your mug refl ects the light of the fl ames. Th is orange 
dance mesmerizes you, taking you back in time. But here in the 
present—the inn door bursts open as a group of young men rush 
into the hall. Th eir voices overlap, agitated and eager to be the 
fi rst to spread the news of the day: the Holy Roman Empire is 
marching to invade the Papal States. From outside, you hear 
loud voices; people are out in the streets discussing the news. You 
make your way to the notice board where a herald is posting 
a proclamation calling for “volunteers” from the population of 
Rome. Someone in the crowd recognizes you and, knowing of 
your deeds, cries out to you, hoping to convince you to respond 
to the call. Th e herald approaches you and says, “If you’re really 
interested, report to this address tomorrow, and you will receive 
all the information you need.” Th e events of the following days 
are turbulent and accompanied by growing fear amongst the 
Roman population. Th e army, composed of Landsknechts and 
other mercenaries, is advancing every day. No one seems able to 
stop it; indeed, many duchies and cities have provided support to 
the invader for the sole purpose of not attracting his wrath. Now, 
by order of Monsignor Ennio Filonardi, you and your group 
must leave the Eternal City to join Giovanni dalle Bande Nere, 
son of Giovanni de Medici and Caterina Sforza, the Lioness of 
Forlì.
You turn your homes upside down in search of equipment from 
your old adventures, but unfortunately many of your belongings 
now too worn out, while you got rid of others long ago. You 
decide to stop by the market to get what you need before setting 
off for Mantua, using the money you received from the prelate as 
an advance on your salary.

How to use this expansion
Under Siege adds a new campaign that is best played 
aft er completing the Core Game and the fi rst Nova Aetas 
Renaissance expansion: Vesuvius. You can continue playing 
with the Heroes you played with in the previous campaign, 
or, if you prefer, you can create new Heroes to play with in 
this expansion’s campaign (see the Skip the Past section).
To play the Under Siege Campaign, in addition to the material 
contained in this expansion, you will need many components 
from the core game. Th ese will be indicated within the 
Mission Cards and the paragraphs of the Plotbook, as usual.
In the sections of this Rulebook, you will fi nd all the new 
rules and components introduced by this expansion.
Th e Under Siege Campaign takes place many years aft er the 
events of Th e League of Cambrai (the Core Game Campaign), 
and those narrated in Vesuvius. During the time since your 
last adventure, your heroes have hung up their swords and 
settled down to the quieter life in Rome. 
Now that they have been called back into action, unfortunately 
they discover that much of their equipment has been lost 
or worn out. To depict this setback, please go through the 
following steps before beginning the Under Siege Campaign:
◆ Delete all reagents noted on the Campaign Sheet used for 
your previous campaign.
◆ Each Hero begins the Under Siege Campaign with only 
their Bond Equipment (if they have any) and one other 
piece of equipment of their choice from those noted on 
the Campaign Sheet. (Th is includes any  gained during 
previous campaigns). Please consider what you might 
want to do with any Basic Class Equipment, as it cannot 
be craft ed again (since it doesn’t have a Formulae Card).
◆ All Equipment noted on the Campaign Sheet that has 
not been selected by any Hero must be discarded. All 
Equipment craft ed using Formulas is returned to the 
Formulae deck and can be craft ed again by visiting the 
Artisan and spending the required Reagents.

Hailing from the frozen regions of Northern Europe, 
Brynhildr is a mystical warrior who draws her strength and 
abilities from ancient Norse traditions. She is a member of 
the mysterious “Sisterhood of the Spear,” an all-female order 
dedicated to the art of spear fi ghting, who strive to pass on 
the techniques of this unique weapon to future generations. 
What could she possibly be doing in Italy, so far from home?
Brynhildr is a new Hero who can be used in any Nova Aetas 
Renaissance campaign.
Brynhildr can also be used as a Companion, using the 
corresponding card and following the normal rules of the 
Nova Aetas Renaissance core game.

Travel and Rest Phases
Th e Under Siege Campaign is mainly set in Rome, so there 
is no Travel Phase. You will be reminded of this at the end of 
each Mission.
Each Mission in the core game lists, under its title, the name 
of the place where you will rest and the type of location where 
this phase will take place, i.e. whether it is a village or a city.
In Under Siege, instead of the name of the place, you can fi nd 
the name of the district of Rome where you will carry out 
the Rest Phase and a list of icons related to the stores in that 
district. Th ese will be the only activities available during the 
next Rest Phase.

Recipes and Cooking
In Under Siege you will have at your disposal the Menu, 
which is a sheet from which you can access a series of recipes. 
Making these will allow you to avoid Starvation (see next 
section).
All possible Recipes are listed in the Menu and are divided 
into 3 categories: Camp Kitchen Dishes, Special Dishes, and 
Chef ’s Dishes.
Cooking
Camp Kitchen Dishes are always available to all Heroes, and 
allow them to avoid the penalty infl icted by the Starvation 
rule. Special Dishes and Chef ’s Dishes are only available 
to Heroes who have acquired specifi c Cooking Skills. In 
addition to allowing Heroes to avoid Starvation, Special and 
Chef ’s Dishes also provide additional Eff ects. Th ese can be 
utilized in the Mission that the Heroes embark on aft er the 
Rest Phase during which they make the Recipe.

To make Recipes during a Rest Phase, you must fi rst acquire 
the Bivouac Token. If you don’t have the Token, no Hero

3 Elementum Reagents: Wood, removing them from the

end of a Mission, in which case the Plotbook will indicate 
that “Th e party gains the Bivouac Token.” Th e token can then 
be used in the next Rest Phase. Place the Bivouac Token on 
the Menu when you gain it, as a reminder. Once you have the 
Bivouac Token, each Hero can make as many Recipes as they 
have Ingredients for at the end of the current Rest Phase.
Remember: Elementum Reagent: Wood can be purchased 
at the Market during the Rest Phase.
Follow these rules when making a Recipe:
◆ When you use an Ingredient to create a Recipe, remove it 
from the Campaign Sheet.
◆ Each Hero who wishes to cook during a Rest Phase selects 
1 or more Recipes that they are able to cook from the 
Menu.
◆ Each Hero can use their Cooking Skills and apply the 
Eff ects of the Recipes they cook only on themselves.
◆ A Hero can make multiple Recipes to gain diff erent 
bonuses. Fully resolve the Eff ects of 1 Recipe before 
making the next.
◆ Each Recipe can be cooked by multiple Heroes, as long as 
the required Ingredients are available. 
Recipe Effects
Recipes have an Eff ect that must be applied immediately aft er 
removing the required Ingredients from the Campaign Sheet. 
Th e Eff ects of a Recipe follow these rules:
Automatic Eff ect: Must be applied immediately aft er use. 
Triggered Eff ect: Eff ects that will only come into play if 
you obtain specifi c symbols. Th e Eff ect can be Positive or 
Negative. Rolling the dice for these Eff ects is mandatory.
Th e bonuses provided by a Recipe’s Eff ect (including not 
suff ering Starvation) apply only to the Mission following the 
Rest Phase in which the Recipe is made.
Starvation
If a Hero fails to gain the “Ignore Starvation” bonus during a 
Rest Phase, they suff er the eff ects of Starvation.
A Hero suff ering from Starvation must roll a  and apply 
the following:
◆ No Symbol: -1 penalty on the  Characteristic.
◆ Star: -1 penalty on the  Characteristic.
◆ Sun: -1 penalty on the  Characteristic.
◆ Moon: -1 penalty on the  Characteristic.
Th is Starvation penalty remains in eff ect until the start of the 
next Rest Phase.
Cooking Skills
Under Siege introduces a new type of Skill that all Heroes can 
learn during this campaign. All available Cooking Skills are 
listed in the Menu.
During the Rest Phase, each Hero who has  available may 
spend them to purchase Cooking Skills, along with the Skills 
available on their Skill Sheets.
On the new Under Siege Campaign Sheet each Hero has 3 
new slots in the Skills section to mark the Cooking Skills they 
have learned. Each Hero can learn a maximum of 3 Cooking 
Skills. All learned Cooking Skills can be used during any Rest 
Phase, without needing to be equipped like Class Skills.
Each Cooking Skill is individual and can’t be used to cook for 
others, unless otherwise specifi ed.
