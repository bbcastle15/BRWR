A new Titan has awoken in the troubled 
Italian lands. Hyperion, the Lord of the 
Flame, has been summoned by a mysterious 
cult for a sole purpose – to purify the 
world with the power of fire. However, 
by freeing the Titan’s immense 
power, the cult set in motion a 
series of events that will involve 
the uncanny Lodge of the Black 
Rose, which Hyperion has always 
desired to rule. It will be up to you 
to face this new menace!

How to use this Expansion
This Expansion adds a new Side Campaign to be included in any Nova 
Aetas Renaissance Campaign of the Core Game or any Expansion.
This Side Campaign comprises one Encounter Side Mission and a 
Hunt Mission, to be played in order starting from Mission H1 - The 
Ritual.
If you decide to use Hyperion in your Campaign, add the Event Card 
found in this box to the Campaign Event Card Deck.
If you draw the Event Card “A World in Flames” during a Travel 
Phase, you may decide to undertake it like a normal Side Mission.
Once you begin this Side Campaign, you must play the 2 S Missions 
it is made up of consecutively, starting from Mission H1 - The Ritual.
At the end of the Encounter Mission, you might have to abandon the 
Side Campaign.
If this happens, put the Event Card “A World in Flames” back in the 
Event Card deck and play the next Main Campaign Mission.
You can undertake this Side Campaign again in the future, but if you 
have never completed it before you must always begin from Mission 
H1 - The Ritual.
After playing Mission H2, no matter the result, follow the regular 
rules to resume the Nova Aetas Renaissance Campaign. If you want 
to face Hyperion again in the future, you must only play Mission H2, 
without playing the whole Side Campaign again.
Mark this Hunt among the available ones on the Campaign Sheet.
Quitting the Side Campaign
Completing the Side Campaign
Mission H2 of this Side Campaign is a Hunt Mission and follows all 
the rules for this type of Mission, described in the core Rulebook of 
Nova Aetas Renaissance.

Hyperion Injuries
Some Models occupy more than a single Battlefield Square. These 
Models are called Large Models. These Models occupy only a single 
Square during movement. In other words, while moving, a Large 
Model is considered as a normal Model, and then returns to occupying 
more Squares the moment the movement ends. When players need 
to determine the movement of a Large Model, they choose any of the 
Squares occupied by the Model and count starting from the chosen 
Square (a normal-sized Model can be used to simulate the movement 
of the Large Model). When the Large Model finishes its movement, 
the players place the Model so that the Squares occupied include the 
Square where it finished its movement. If placement is not possible 
because the required Squares (usually 4) are not all available, players 
refer to the following rules:
Large Models
 A Large Model is considered to be targeted by an Attack with the 
Area Trait if at least one of the Squares it occupies is within the Area 
of the Attack.
If a Large Model is the target of an Attack with the Area Trait, it 
suffers only the Effect intended for the target, even if it occupies 
multiple Squares within the Area. 
If a Large Model is not the target of an Attack with the Area Trait 
and occupies more than one Square within the Area, it suffers the 
additional Effect only once.

H1 - The Ritual
H1-1
You decide to dig deeper into the words of the poor people you have 
met. You head to their village and discover that the population is 
being harassed by a group of bandits who have found safe refuge in 
the nearby forest at the base of a hill.
The village chief, a certain Luca da Riace, tells you that this group 
of criminals calls themselves "The Flaming Hand." They wear 
predominantly red clothes and some of them proudly display burns 
on their bodies or faces.
The village chief offers to pay you a good sum of money if you can 
drive them off their lands...
... Then he leaves, muttering to himself about a quick change of jobs.

H1-2
In the heart of the forest, you come across a marshy clearing that 
blocks your path. You are considering how to cross it without 
becoming stiff terracotta soldiers when a figure appears from behind 
a tree between you and the marsh.
The man gives you a stunned look—it's clear he wasn't expecting 
visitors—then he recovers, quickly fastens his pants and, putting 
two fingers to his mouth, lets out a deafening whistle.
Only now do you notice his clothing in shades of red and his 
disfigured face. It seems that the surprise effect has faded. Three 
individuals armed with long rifles rush from the woods beyond the 
swamp.

You warily approach the pyre in the center of the scorched clearing, 
with the flames continuing to burn with an unnatural brilliance 
even now that there are no more followers of the Flaming Hand.
Some tongues of fire appear to form a figure, but the light suddenly 
becomes too strong for you to keep your eyes open. You are 
enveloped in an intense but restorative heat, which a few moments 
later vanishes as if it were sucked into the pyre.
When you manage to open your eyes again, the pyre has disappeared 
and a huge figure stands out in front of you, spreading its enormous 
wings and lowering a fiery gaze upon you.
You must face it if you want to get back to the village.
