The tiny hamlet of Borgo di Civita di 
Bagnoregio hides a dangerous secret. 
Its inhabitants speak of a powerful, 
ancient creature, seemingly bound to 
the mountain that hosts the village. 
Nobody has ever seen it clearly, but 
everyone is convinced it exists. 
It is called “The Devil of 
Bagnoregio.” Are you ready to 
find out the true nature of 
this Devil?

This Expansion adds a new Side Campaign to be included in any Nova 
Aetas Renaissance Campaign of the Core Game or any Expansion.
This Side Campaign comprises two Encounter Side Missions and a 
Hunt Mission, to be played in order starting from Mission D1 - An 
Incredible Discovery.
If you decide to use the Draco in your Campaign, add the Event Card 
found in this box to the Campaign Event Card Deck.
If you draw the Event Card “I Saw a Devil” during a Travel Phase, you 
can decide to undertake it like a normal Side Mission.
Once you begin this Side Campaign, you must play the 3 Side 
Missions it is made up of consecutively, starting from Mission 
D1 - An Incredible Discovery.
How to use this Expansion
At the end of Mission D1 and D2 you might have to abandon the 
Side Campaign.
If this happens, put the Event Card “I Saw a Devil” back in the Event 
Card deck and play the next Main Campaign Mission.
You can undertake this Side Campaign again in the future, but if you 
have never completed it before you must always begin from Mission 
D1 - An Incredible Discovery.
Mission D3 of this Side Campaign is a Hunt Mission and follows all 
the rules for this type of Mission, described in the core Rulebook of 
Nova Aetas Renaissance.
Quitting the Side Campaign
Hunt Mission

After playing Mission D3, no matter the result, follow the normal 
rules to resume the Nova Aetas Renaissance Campaign. If you want 
to face the Draco again in the future, you must only play Mission D3, 
without playing the whole Side Campaign again.
Mark this Hunt among the available ones on the Campaign Sheet if at 
least one Hero visits the Inn during a Rest Phase.
Completing the Side Campaign
Instead of waiting to draw the Event Card "I Saw a Devil," at any point 
in the Main Campaign you can mark the title of this Side Mission in 
the Active Side Mission Slot and play it as if you had drawn the Event 
Card "I Saw a Devil."
Bounty
Some Models occupy more than a single Battlefield Square. These 
Models are called Large Models. These Models occupy only a single 
Square during movement. In other words, while moving, a Large 
Model is considered as a normal Model, and then returns to occupying 
more Squares the moment the movement ends. When players need 
to determine the movement of a Large Model, they choose any of the 
Squares occupied by the Model and count starting from the chosen 
Square (a normal-sized Model can be used to simulate the movement 
of the Large Model). When the Large Model finishes its movement, 
the players place the Model so that the Squares occupied include the 
Square where it finished its movement. If placement is not possible 
because the required Squares (usually 4) are not all available, players 
refer to the following rules:
◆ If there are Models occupying the Squares, the Large Model pushes 
the Models occupying them into adjacent available Squares or the 
nearest available Square.
◆ If there are Scenic Elements occupying Squares, place the Large 
Model in Squares that are available or occupied by other Models. 
If this is not possible, the Large Model ends its Movement in a 
Square that allows its placement.
Large Models
A Large Model is considered to be targeted by an Attack with the 
Area Trait if at least one of the Squares it occupies is within the Area 
of the Attack.
If a Large Model is the target of an Attack with the Area Trait, it 
suffers only the Effect intended for the target, even if it occupies 
multiple Squares within the Area. 
If a Large Model is not the target of an Attack with the Area Trait 
and occupies more than one Square within the Area, it suffers the 
additional Effect only once.
A Large Model always has a valid Line of Sight on all other Models 
and vice versa.

D2 - An Unexpected Encounter
D2-1
The warm welcome you receive at the inn in Civita di Borgoregio 
vanishes when you start asking the folks who want to thank you for 
your efforts information about the map you stumbled into. Someone 
confirms it is a map of this area, although worn out and incomplete, 
but once they notice the sentence “The Devil protects us and protects 
its interests” they get annoyed and walk away, shaking their head. 
“Eeeheheheh—are you here to hunt the Devil?” A man with 
streaked hair and a hollowed face drew near you table. “You’re 
already dead then! You don’t know it yet, but you’re dead!” Before 
you can answer, he points something on the map: “This big, split 
stone is its lair! I know it well, being that I come from Elsewhere.”
While you still try to focus that point of the map, the man leaves 
the inn. You are thinking of getting up and follow him, but the 
innkeeper brings you back to reality. “Pay no heed to him,” he says 
dropping mugs full of foaming beer on the table. “Ol’ Nando has 
a screw loose. He’s been living here for many years but he’s always 
been—extravagant, let’s say.”
Yet, your instincts tell you there is more than just madness in 
that man. Curiosity has the best of you—you leave the beverages 
untouched and hurriedly go for the door, hoping to find him 
outside. You immerse yourselves in a foggy evening. A dense mist 
blurs the houses’ contours and you cannot find your bearings in this 
unfamiliar village. Shadows move around you, but when you get 
closer you realize they are only illusions created by the mist.
Suddenly, you hear the old man’s laughter. You try to follow it, but 
a sound unlike any you have ever heard makes you turn—a huge 
shadow moves in the square, sniffing the air, looking for something.
The Devil is here!
