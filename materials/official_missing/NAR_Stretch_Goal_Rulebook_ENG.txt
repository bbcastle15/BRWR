Familiars
A Familiar is a small animal companion that Heroes can 
purchase at the Market during a Rest Phase, by paying the 
cost in Florins indicated on its card.
Familiars follow these rules:
◆ A Hero may equip a Familiar for a Mission exactly as if it 
were an Equipment Card that takes up no Encumbrance 
Slots.
◆ Each Hero can equip only one Familiar. 
◆ At the beginning of a Mission, place the Familiar's 
Model in the same Square as the Hero who equipped it, 
with their bases in contact. 
◆ A Hero may spend 1 to move their Familiar's Model 
up to 3 Squares, ignoring any Terrain e ects (e.g.,

Large Models and Line of Sight
A Large Model is considered to be targeted by an Attack 
with the Area Trait if at least one of the Squares it occupies 
is within the Area of the Attack.
If a Large Model is the target of an Attack with the Area 
Trait, it su ers only the E ect intended for the target, even 
if it occupies multiple Squares within the Area. 
If a Large Model is not the target of an Attack with the Area 
Trait and occupies more than one Square within the Area, 
it su ers the additional E ect only once.
A Large Model always has a valid Line of Sight on all other 
Models and vice versa
Large Models
Some Models occupy more than a single Battle eld 
Square.  ese Models are called Large Models.  ese 
Models occupy only a single Square during movement. In 
other words, while moving, a Large Model is considered 
as a normal Model, and then returns to occupying more 
Squares the moment the movement ends. When players 
need to determine the movement of a Large Model, they 
choose any of the Squares occupied by the Model and count 
starting from the chosen Square (a normal-sized Model 
can be used to simulate the movement of the Large Model). 
When the Large Model  nishes its movement, the players 
place the Model so that the Squares occupied include the 
Square where it  nished its movement. If placement is not 
possible because the required Squares (usually 4) are not all 
available, players refer to the following rules:
◆ If there are Models occupying the Squares, the Large 
Model pushes the Models occupying them into adjacent 
available Squares or the nearest available Square.
◆ If there are Scenic Elements occupying Squares, place 
the Large Model in Squares that are available or occupied 
by other Models. If this is not possible, the Large Model 
ends its Movement in a Square that allows its placement.
