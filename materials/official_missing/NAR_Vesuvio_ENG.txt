You have been contacted on behalf of King Ferdinand of 
Aragon: word of your exploits has reached Naples.
Recently, the Vesuvius region was struck by a violent 
earthquake, causing havoc in the local communities. 
As if that weren't enough, bandits are terrorizing the 
citizens with constant raids, robberies, and violence.
If it were a simple gang of criminals, there would be 
no need to call for your intervention, but these bandits 
are numerous and well-organized, able to evade and 
in some cases even overpower the local militia. Th eir 
presence has plunged the people into despair and has 
undermined the role of the authorities, who have been 
judged incapable of dealing with this disaster.
Th is is why the king turns to you, hoping that someone 
from outside the kingdom can look at the problem with 
a fresh perspective and fi nd a solution. And who better 
than a group of seasoned heroes like you?
You decide to seize the opportunity to make another ally 
on the peninsula, and set out with what little information 
you have gathered about the bandits—your search must 
be discreet to avoid backlash, and you must focus on the 
slopes of Mount Somma, the mountain complex that is 
home to Mount Vesuvius. Th e criminals operate under a 
kind of banner, the emblem of a scarred eye...

How to use this expansion
Th e Vesuvius expansion adds a new campaign that is best 
played aft er completing the Core Nova Aetas Renaissance 
Campaign. You can continue to use the Heroes you 
completed the main Campaign with, or, if you wish, you 
can create new Heroes to play the Campaign with (see the 
Skipping the Past section below).
To play the Vesuvius Campaign, you will need many 
components of the Core Game in addition to the materials 
included in this expansion. Th ese are listed as usual in the 
Mission Cards and Plotbook sections.
Th is rulebook list all of the new rules and components 
introduced by this expansion.
You can start a new campaign directly from the Vesuvius 
Expansion with new Heroes, without having to play (or 
replay) the League of Cambrais campaign from the Nova 
Aetas Renaissance Core Game.
If you choose to do so, follow these rules. Create four new 
Heroes following the rules of the Core Game. Each Hero 
can thus spend up to 13  Experience Points. You can 
spend these  to purchase Skills and acquire an Advanced 
Class, always keeping in mind any character development 
restrictions such as Skills prerequisites or having to choose 
at least 3 Basic Skills before you can choose an Advanced 
Class for the character (see the Campaign section of the 
Rulebook).
Skipping the Past
To equip the party, you have at your disposal a common 
pool of 110  Florins to be used to purchase Reagents and 
create Formulae Equipment Cards. In addition, 2 Heroes 
of the players' choice may, provided they have acquired an 
Advanced Class, read all of their Destiny Cards and related 
paragraphs of the Plotbook, and receive both the Bound 
Advanced Equipment Card and the Advanced Equipment 
Card normally given as a reward for resolving the fourth 
Destiny Card. Th e remaining Heroes, again only if they 
have acquired an Advanced Class, may read the Destiny 
Cards of their Basic Class and the fi rst Destiny Card of 
the chosen Advanced Class, which must be resolved while 
playing. As per the rules, these Heroes receive the Bound 
Advanced Equipment of the chosen Advanced Class.

Landscape Cards
Th e Vesuvius Expansion introduces a new type of Mission 
Cards, called Landscape. Th ese cards function similarly 
to the Location Cards of the Core Game, but are tied to a 
specifi c Tile. When you reveal a Landscape card, place it 
next to the indicated Tile.
Landscape Cards depict the landscape a Hero has in front 
of them and one or more Scenic Elements present on the 
Tile it is linked to.
Unlike with Location Cards, Heroes cannot be placed on 
Landscape Cards—you can interact with a Landscape Card 
from anywhere on the linked Tile.
Th e Interactions of a Landscape Card normally cost 0
since the Hero is simply looking at their surroundings. 
Further explanation in the Plotbook will indicate how you 
can interact with the illustrated Scenic Elements.
Landscape Cards can provide useful clues to the mission 
at hand, so we advise you to pay close attention to the 
elements illustrated on them.

Master Peio
No one knows his real name, but this particular character 
calls himself Master Peio. 
Only three things are known about him—he is a very 
skilled forger, he always carries excellent equipment to sell, 
and he is from Resina, although he loves to travel around 
the Peninsula, frequenting the markets between Florence 
and Naples in search of new ideas and opportunities. 
Some Missions in this Expansion will give the opportunity 
to "Access the services of Master Peio" during a Rest Phase. 
Th is option is listed in the End of Mission directions.
Accessing the services of Master Peio counts as visiting a 
Shop. Master Peio can create Master Peio's Glyph Cards 
and Formulae Equipment Cards. Th ese are also marked
