Credits
The Shrine of the Teutonic Order is a long abandoned site.
Its exact location is unknown.
Once a symbol of power and grandeur, it is now a shell of its former glory, and so is its last host.
The Order deliberately built it in a secluded location to secretly guard the remains of the greatest Knights, those 
who had distinguished themselves by their abilities.
And so the Shrine became the final resting place for many Grand Magisters.
Ulrich was the last Guest, not by merit, but by his own specific order.
Defeated in the Battle of Grunwald, he was left to die locked up in the Shrine, as he wished.
Since then, the Shrine has been abandoned by the Order. No one dares to set foot there again.
Thanks to the knowledge of dark magic, Ulrich's spirit survives in his weathered body.
A few fools have attempted to breach the Shrine, now adding to the ranks of the undead.
Venturing there could open the door to great power, but are you sure you want to pay the price?

HOW TO READ THIS RULEBOOK
This rulebook is divided into six chapters, which are to be consulted as indicated below in order to understand the game as simply 
and straightforwardly as possible.
1. Components: this chapter visually presents all the game components in the box.
2. Basics: this chapter introduces the game without going into the rules, but rather provides a foundation for better 
understanding the following chapters.
3. Setup: this chapter contains all the instructions for setting up the game area.
4. Game Turn: this chapter explains in detail the rules that come into play during the five phases that make up a game turn, 
clarifying the flow of actions that each player must put into practice, but without going into detail about rules that are not 
strictly related to a single game Phase, all of which are recalled by cross-references and directions to the chapter that contains 
them.
5. End of Game Tally: this chapter covers the rules for determining the winner at the end of the game.
6. Rules Compendium: this chapter explains in detail all the in-depth rules that affect the game continuously or that require 
a temporary interruption of the game flow in order to be applied.
7. Beyond the Duel: this chapter provides practical instructions for using this product in combination with the other 
products in the Black Rose Wars line.

Models and Bases
Bartholomäus
Ulrich
Bartholomäus is the leader of the dark occultist coven known as 
Totentanz.
Traditionally, the coven leader chooses their successor and trains 
them, until the latter defeats the Master and takes their place.
When this happens, the new leader buries their mentor and takes 
their mask, forever concealing their identity from the eyes of the 
rest of the coven.
Behind Bartholomäus' mask anyone can hide, man or woman, 
young or old.
Only one thing is certain: their total dedication to the cause.
This is why Bartholomäus is now on a quest to find the lost Shrine 
of the Order of the Teutonic Knights. Reports tell of an enormous 
power held in its rooms. A power capable of summoning hosts of 
Templars under its control—the power of the Lex school of magic!
Ulrich von Jungingen was the 26th Grand Master of the Teutonic 
Order.
In a mad quest for power he practiced dark magic throughout his 
life, hoping to make history and be remembered forever.
At the height of his power he created the obscure school of magic 
known as Lex Intoxicated with power,he decided to unleash the 
Teutonic Order against Poland and Lithuania, convinced that 
he could easily subjugate them, but his thirst for power made him 
arrogant, and in 1410 A.D. he lost his life in battle.
Because of his dark practices, his remains now restlessly lie in a 
shrine in his homeland. 
He continues to defend his legacy, and will do so until a brave one 
succeeds in defeating him by taking away his dark power.

Basics
Black Rose Wars Duel: Lex is a game of mage duels set in the Black Rose 
Lodge, a sentient place where a powerful supernatural being, the Black 
Rose, resides. 
In this place, the most powerful mages in the world gather, learn, and 
challenge each other to determine who is the best among them, the one 
who will become the next Grand Magister of the Lodge.
This game allows two players to compete in a terrifying battle of magic 
and teaches the fundamentals of this game's older brother, Black Rose 
Wars. Everything in this box can be used with Black Rose Wars to 
expand the core game. 
But it doesn't stop there: Schools of Magic, Rooms and Mages from 
any product in the Black Rose Wars line can be used in place of the 
materials in this box to create ever-changing duels using the rules 
described in this rulebook.

The Lodge and the Rooms
The Mage represents the player during the game. Each Mage has 
their own Model representing them in the Lodge and a Mage Card 
describing their characteristics.
Please note: On all game components, players are identified with 
the term "Mage."
The Lodge is the battleground where the mages duel during the 
game. The Lodge consists of 7 Rooms, which are placed on the 
board during game setup.
A Room has two states, Destroyed or Rebuilt. The side with the 
slots (called Instability Slots, see page 18) represents the Destroyed 
side and the reverse side represents Rebuilt Rooms.
The specific rules for Rooms and their Effects are explained in 
detail on page 19.

Mages use Spell Cards to unleash powerful spells in the Lodge.
There are three types of Spell Cards: School Spell Cards, Personal Spell 
Cards and Forgotten Spell Cards.
SCHOOL SPELLS
The School of Magic (Lex in this box) consists of a Deck of 36 School 
Spell Cards (12 different Spell Cards, 3 copies each). Its deck constitutes 
the Library.
PERSONAL SPELLS
Each Mage comes with three copies of their Personal Spell Card. 
The illustration shows the Mage the cards are associated with.
Each player is given 25 of these cubes of a specific color. The 25 black￾colored cubes are used to track effects related to the Black Rose, the 
entity that dwells in the Lodge and participates in the game as the third 
player.
The Game Effects indicate whether to use a Damage/Instability 
Cube to represent a  Damage, the wounds the Mage inflicts on 
the opponent and the Evocations they control, or to represent 
Instability, magical energy that the Mage releases in the Lodge Rooms 
to gain control of them.
Damage/Instability Cubes are also used during the game for other 
purposes besides those just described such as, for example, keeping 
track on the game board of what turn is in progress or how many times 
you have defeated your opponent.

These two slots on the game board house the Mages' Models at the 
beginning of the game. Each Mage is matched to the Cell on their 
player's side of the game board (recognizable by color).
When defeated, a Mage is placed in their Cell.
See page 19 for Cell's complete rules. 
The Event Card indicates Effects that the Black Rose applies at the 
beginning of each Game Turn to influence the game and try to win 
(see page 10).
Quest Cards show six  Quests available to players to collect additional

Setup
Follow the steps below to set up the game:
1 Open the game board A in the center of the Game Area.
2 Place the Occult Black Rose Room B in the center of the 
game board so that the Destroyed side is visible, that is, the side 
showing the Instability Slots (the holes).
Shuffle the remaining six Rooms and place them randomly 
around the Occult Black Rose Room so that the Destroyed side 
is visible; the set of seven Rooms is called the Lodge.
3 Place the Events Card C in the corresponding slot on the 
game board, so that one of the two sides chosen randomly or 
by mutual agreement is visible. Place the two Quest Cards D
in the corresponding slots of the game board, so that one of the 
two randomly or mutually chosen faces of each is visible.
4 Place the Room Activation Tokens E and the Black Rose's 
Damage/Instability Cubes (black cubes) F next to the 
Game Board, so that they are within reach of both players.
5 The player who last received a rose (or a randomly chosen 
player) gains the Crown Token () G , becoming the 
Crown Holder.
6 Starting with the Crown Holder and proceeding clockwise, 
each player chooses a color and sits on the side of the game board 
showing that color. They then take the Damage/Instability 
Cubes, Physical Action Tokens, Persistence Tokens, Power 
Token, and Evocation Bases corresponding to that color H . 
The player then chooses a Mage, takes the corresponding Model
and places it in the Cell I of their color. They take their Mage 
Card and place it on the corresponding slot on their side of the 
game board and place their Physical Action Tokens on it J . 
then they take their Mage's three Personal Spell Cards. K and 
keeps them in front of them, revealed.
7 Take the Lex Spell School deck and place it next to the game 
board so that it is within reach of all players. Place the School 
Reference Card L under the School of Magic deck as shown 
in the image. This deck is considered the Library M .
Shuffle the available Forgotten Spells and create a deck; place 
it next to the game board in the corresponding slot N .
Place the Models of Evocations, 3 Templarii, within reach O .
8 The Crown Holder takes the School Reference Card L
from the Library and selects one of the two Starting Grimoires 
E indicated on it. The player searches the School of Magic 
deck for the six cards indicated, then adds to them a Personal 
Spell Card of their own Mage and shuffles them. This seven￾card deck is their Grimoire; they place it in the corresponding 
slot on their side of the game board () P , Then they take 
their other two Personal Spell Cards and place them revealed 
in the Memories slot () of their side of the game board Q . 
The other player does the same, however, choosing the Starting 
Grimoire that the Crown Holder did not choose.
Once this process is completed, shuffle the rest of the School of 
Magic deck of the Library and place it back on the side of the 
game board above the School Reference Card. 
9 Each player places one of their Damage/Instability Cube on slot 
0 of the Trophy Track and Room Track of their side of the 
game board R ; then places their own Power Token on slot 0 of 
the Power Track S .
The Crown Holder places a Damage/Instability Cube of the 
Black Rose on slot 0 of the Trophy Track, Room Track, and 
Turn Track of the game board T ; then they place the Black 
Rose Power Token on square 0 of the Power Track U .
Now you are ready to play.

EFFECTS
The game often refers to Effects. Most components of the game 
and every Physical Action (see page 13) have Effects, which are 
composed of one or more phrases and/or symbols that allow 
you to influence the game. Each phrase that makes up an Effect

TARGETS
The Effects of Spells and other 
components of the game always have 
a specific target. That target must be 
chosen from those available by the player 
resolving the Effect. 
The target of an Effect is always indicated 
before the text of the Effect itself followed 
by a colon (:), except for the Spell Card 
Effect targets, which are listed in the 
third banner under the Spell name.
Effects resolved by a Mage with 
targets other than , unless specified 
in their text, never harm the Mage themselves and/or their 
Evocations. 
Targets may be:
•  Model: the Effect can target a Model other than itself. 
The number next to the icon (, , ) indicates the 
range, i.e., the number of Rooms of distance from the 
Model resolving the Effect in which the target might be; to 
choose a target, it must be possible to draw a straight line 
not blocked by the walls of the Rooms between the Room 
in which the target is and the Room in which the Model 
resolving the Effect is. If the icon is followed by an asterisk 
(), the Effect can target a Model in the Lodge regardless 
of distance, and even if an unbroken straight line cannot be 
drawn as indicated above.
•  Mage Model: as Model , but only a Mage's Model can 
be targeted.
•  Evocation Model: as Model , but only an Evocation's 
Model can be targeted.
•  Room: the Effect targets a Room and affects all 
Opposing Models (Mages and their Evocations) within that 
Room. The number within the icon (, , ) indicates 
the range, i.e., the number of Rooms away from the 
Model applying the Effect to the target Room; to choose 
the target Room, it must be possible to draw a straight 
line not blocked by the walls of the Rooms between it and 
the Room in which the Model resolving the Effect is. If 
the icon contains an asterisk (), the Effect can target a 
Lodge Room regardless of distance and even if an unbroken 
straight line cannot be drawn as indicated above.
•  Personal: This Effect targets the Mage who resolves it.
•  Special: no specific target. The text of the Effect 
indicates how to apply it and which elements of the game 
are affected by it.
For examples and in-depth rules on Targets, Range and 
Line of Sight see page 20.

RO O M AC T I VAT I O N TO K E N S
When a Room in which there is a Room Activation Token

Rooms and Room Activation Tokens
These two slots on the game board house the Mages' Models at the 
beginning of the game. Each Mage is matched to the Cell on their 
player's side of the game board (recognizable by color).
Cells and the Mages within them use the following rules:
• No Model can move, be moved or placed in a Cell; only when a 
Mage is defeated (see page 18) is their Model placed in their Cell. 
• A Mage in a Cell may exclusively perform the following:
▶ Activate their Evocations during the Evocation Phase.
▶ Perform the Momentum Action.
▶ Perform the Explore Action.
• When a Mage begins their Activation in a Cell, they must
perform an Explore Action or a Momentum Action to get 
out of it. If they cannot do so, they remain in the Cell.
• A Mage in a Cell is not considered to be in the Lodge and cannot 
be targeted by Effects or affected by anything, except for Events 
Card Effects, Quest Cards (see pages 7) and Effects that specify 
that they apply to a Mage in their Cell.

RANGE
The numerical value of the targets (see page 11) is called the range, 
and it indicates the distance at which the target chosen to apply the 
Effect can be located. Ranges with a value greater than 0 ( ) 
require line-of-sight between the person resolving the Effect and 
the target.
A  range ( ) or one with a value of 0 ( ) instead, does 
not require any line of sight.
LINE OF SIGHT
The line of sight is an imaginary, straight line between the center of 
the Room in which the Model resolving the Effect is located and the 
center of the Room in which the target of the Effect or the Room 
targeted by the Effect is located. This line must always run through 
the centers of a row of Rooms, and can never run along the edge of 
a Room traversing a maximum number of Rooms (excluding the 
starting Room) equal to the range indicated on the Effect's target.
DUMMY TARGET
You can choose a Dummy Target as the target of an Effect that 
targets Models (). The Dummy Target counts as a Model, 
even if it is not physically in the Lodge; the purpose of choosing 
it as a target is to allow Mages to cast a Spell and resolve as much 
of its Effect as possible, even when there are no Models available 
as targets. A Dummy Target does not suffer Damage. It is not 
possible to choose a Dummy Model as the target of a Physical 
Attack. ().

D U E L I N TO B L AC K RO S E WA R S
The Material in this box can be used to expand Black Rose Wars by 
including it in games as shown below.
Lex School of Magic: during step 9 of the Setup you may replace 
one of the Schools of Magic present in the core game with the Lex 
School.
Forgotten Spells: you may add these cards to the Forgotten Spells 
deck of the core game. 
Bartholomäus and Ulrich: these two Mages can be chosen as a Mage 
during step 8 of the Setup described in the core game rulebook.
Rooms: the Rooms in this box (and the corresponding Activation 
Token) can replace Rooms in the core game that have the same Name 
without regard to the italicized part, to have a Lodge with more 
varied Effects; for example, you can replace the Black Rose Room of 
the core game with the Occult Black Rose Room, or the Armory of 
the core game with the Templar Armory.
B L A C K R O S E WA R S I N T O D U E L 
Material from the other products in the Black Rose Wars line can be 
used to expand this game by including it in games as shown below.
School of Magic: you may replace the Lex School of Magic with any 
School of Magic from the other products, making sure to include in 
the game all the materials necessary for the operation of that School 
(such as Evocations). Due to the head-to-head challenge nature of 
this game, some of the Schools of Magic that develop complex tactics 
may not be impactful in a duel. It should also be kept in mind that if a 
School of Magic involves the use of additional rules, these will need to 
be consulted in the rulebook of the Black Rose Wars core game or in 
the rulebook of the expansion from which the chosen School of Magic 
comes.
Forgotten Spells: you may add any Forgotten Spells from the other 
Black Rose Wars products to the games of Duel by adding them to the 
deck during Setup. 
Mages: you may use all Mages from the other Black Rose Wars 
products in Duel games.
Rooms: you may replace the seven Rooms with Rooms from other 
Black Rose Wars products when playing Duel. 
Incompatible Materials: No Quest Cards or Event Cards from 
Black Rose Wars are included in a Duel game. Materials that generate 
Effects (usually Spells and Rooms) that call upon these types of cards 
must never be included in the game.

B L AC K RO S E WA R S
Black Rose Wars is the first chapter in this thrilling saga. 
The Black Rose, a mysterious sentient being, rules the winds 
of magic and calls upon the best Mages. Every ten years, 
the mages compete in deadly battles to choose the Grand 
Magister. The power of spells and terrifying evocations 
eventually comes to destroy the rooms of the Lodge. Each 
time, however, the magic of the Black Rose repairs the Lodge 
until the next challenge.
INFERNO
Inferno is the expansion that introduces the first Avatar to 
the game, and presents players with a challenge that borders 
on the impossible.
Lucifero has escaped the control of his summoner, and the 
Lodge has been transformed into the Circles of Hell.
In the bloody battle that ensues, the power of Magic will 
be so great as to destroy the Lodge forever, and with it, the 
Order of the Black Rose.
REBIRTH
Black Rose Wars: Rebirth is the second installment of the 
game, a new core box that introduces new game mechanics, 
new Schools of Magic, new Mages, and more. Mages are 
called upon to rebuild the Lodge. The Voice of the Black 
Rose calls to the Perceivers, men and women who can hear 
her faint call. Now it is your duty to rebuild what Lucifero 
has destroyed.
APOCALYPSE
Apocalypse is the expansion that lets you take on the role of 
the Horsemen of the Apocalypse, four of the most incredible 
Mages in history. A breathtaking challenge with pre-built 
decks and a new game mechanic. With this expansion, you 
can also build your own Mages and decks from scratch 
for even faster and more furious games. Apocalypse also 
includes all the rules you need to organize and play real Black 
Rose Wars tournaments.
