Game Objective
In Nova Aetas Renaissance you will play through a Campaign
divided into a series of Missions that puts your tactical skills 
to the test.
Each Mission will evolve the campaign story in diff erent ways 
based on how you complete them.
Th e story will develop based on your choices and actions.
When you reach one of the eight diff erent endings, you will 
have fi nished your adventure, following a compelling story 
told through Mission Cards and the Plotbook.
Th e main goal of the game is to entertain you and tell your 
story. Aft er you have read one of the endings, you can begin 
a new Campaign to explore and discover every secret of the 
fascinating world of Nova Aetas Renaissance!

Golden Rules
If a rule written on any other game component (card, sheet, 
or other) confl icts with the rules described in this rulebook, 
the rule on the game component always has priority.
If during a Test, the Success Th reshold is not specifi ed, the 
Success Th reshold is always 5.
Regardless of any modifi ers to the roll of the die, or to the Test 
being performed, a result of 1 on the die is always a fail while 
a result of 8 is always a success.

Authors' Note: 
Within this Rulebook we try to clarify as many questions as possible regarding most game situations.
Yet in a game as vast as Nova Aetas Renaissance you may come across a situation that we have failed to 
foresee or that the rules fail to clarify entirely. In such cases, we recommend that you use common sense 
to resolve it or—in case you want to keep in the spirit in which we designed this game—always back the 
Enemies up by choosing what is best for them! Functional as it may be, the Artifi cial Intelligence we have 
designed will never compete with a player's brain, so giving advantage to the Enemies is what we would 
do if we were in an uncertain situation.

Playing the Enemies
Nova Aetas Renaissance is a fully cooperative game and its 
game system makes the Enemies very autonomous, so in 
many cases players will simply have to follow the Instructions 
provided by their Artifi cial Intelligence (AI) and move 
miniatures around the Game Area according to the directions 
provided.
However, more complicated situations may occur where 
players will have to make decisions to manage the Enemies in 
the best way. When such situations occur, follow these simple 
rules:
◆ Arti cial Intelligence: Goals set by Missions for Enemies 
always take priority over any other factor.
◆ Be Naughty!: any good story would not be such if there 
were no antagonists equal to the Heroes, able to put them 
in a diffi cult situation and always staying one step ahead of 
them. In dubious situations always make the best choices 
for the Enemies.

Heroes Characteristics
Each Characteristic of a Hero can have a value from 1 to 6 
and represents the character's ability to infl uence a certain 
aspect of the game world: 
Swiftness 
◆ It represents the Hero's refl exes and speed.
◆ It determines the order of Activation of the Hero with 
respect to the other Models.
◆ It provides an equal number of Action Points  to the 
Hero when they become Active.
◆ It determines the number of dice the Hero rolls for Tests 
involving alertness and speed.
Dexterity :
◆ It represents the agility of the Hero. 
◆ It determines the number of dice the Hero rolls for 
Physical Attack Tests with Weapons that use Dexterity 
(usually all ranged Weapons such as rifl es, bows or 
daggers).
◆ It determines the number of dice the Hero rolls for Tests 
involving dexterity and accuracy.
Physique :
◆ It represents the physical prowess and strength of the 
Hero. 
◆ It determines the number of dice the Hero rolls for 
Physical Attack Tests with Weapons that use Physique 
(usually all melee weapons such as swords, axes or spears).
Height Level (HL)
In Nova Aetas Renaissance there are 3 Height Levels:
◆ HL 0: this is the most common level. All Tile Squares that 
make up the Battlefi eld (including W and M Tiles) are 
considered to be at this HL.
◆ HL 1: all Squares on the Roofs of Buildings are considered 
to be at this HL.
◆ HL 2: all Squares on the Tower Roofs are considered to be 
at this HL.
Height Levels follow these rules:
◆ HLs should not be considered when counting Squares 
between two Models. Th erefore, when you need to 
determine whether a Model is within any  or , 
consider Squares on diff erent HLs as being on the same 
HL.
◆ An Attack can target a Model on a HL diff erent from that 
of the Attacking Model only if their  characteristic is 3 
or higher.
◆ Two Models on two diff erent HLs can never be adjacent.
Climbing to a higher HL
By spending 2 , a Model can move from a Square of the 
HL it is in to the nearest Square of a higher HL only in one 
of these cases:
◆ It is in a Square with a Ladder
◆ It has the Climb X Generic Skill (see Handbook) and such 
movement does not cross Squares on the starting Height 
Level.
Descending to a lower HL
By spending 2 , a Model can move from a Square of the HL 
it is in to the nearest Square of a lower HL if that movement 
does not cross Squares on the starting Height Level. Aft er 
performing the movement, the Model gains 2  unless one 
of the following conditions is true:
◆ Th e Model is in a Square of a Roof to which a Ladder is 
leaning.
◆ Th e Model has the Climb X Generic Skill (see Handbook).

KO Hero
When a Hero has a number of Wound Tokens on their Hero 
Sheet equal to their Health Value, they are KO.
A KO Hero follows these rules:
◆ Th e Hero's Model is laid in the Square it is in. Th e Square 
is considered occupied, no Model can stop on it, but all 
Models can pass through it.
◆ If a Hero is KO'd in an occupied Square, their Model is 
moved to an Available Square of the controlling player's 
choice as close as possible to the Square they are in.
◆ A KO Hero is considered out of the game, suff ers no game 
eff ect, cannot be targeted in any way or considered by 
any rule unless it is an eff ect that allows them to discard 
Wound Tokens.
◆ Equipment and Skills of a KO Hero produce no eff ect.
◆ When a Hero is KO'd, their Perilium is 
decreased by 2 points and their Activation 
Token is turned to the KO side.
◆ A KO Hero does not gain Heroic 
Points and/or Class Points.
◆ A KO Hero's Activation Token never leaves the Sector it 
is in.
◆ When a KO Hero would Activate, they instead draw an 
Injury Card.
◆ If an eff ect allows the KO Hero to discard at least one 
Wound Token from their Sheet, they are no longer KO. 
Th eir Model is lift ed into the Square they are in. Th eir 
Activation Token is turned to the regular side and placed 
one Sector ahead of where the Active Model's Activation 
Token is (please note that this is not the Horologium 
Sector following the Active one).
◆ A KO Hero discards all Status Tokens on their Hero Sheet, 
except those on their Equipment Cards.

back of the Reagent cards.
3. Loot: whole. Reveal all the Reagent cards that make up the 
Loot and mark the contents in the Treasury on the Campaign 
Sheet. Th en place the cards back into their decks.

Models Activation
Nova Aetas Renaissance uses a dynamic activation sequence 
that takes into account the amount of time each Action 
performed by a Model takes. To do this, the game exploits 
the Horologium and the Activation Tokens of Heroes and 
Enemies (or groups of Enemies) on it.
Follow these rules to Activate a Model:
◆ When activating a Horologium Sector containing at least 
one or more Activation Tokens, the Models associated 
with them must activate.
◆ Models with the highest Swift ness value  activate fi rst.
◆ In case of a tie among Hero and Enemy Models, the Enemy 
Models activate fi rst.
◆ In case of a tie among Enemies, their order of activation 
is chosen by the players, trying to choose the order of 
activation most advantageous to the Enemies.
◆ In case of a tie among Heroes, their order of activation is 
chosen by the players.

Horologium
Th e Horologium is used to determine the order of activation 
of each Model in the Game Area, to indicate the start and end 
of a Mission and the possible triggering of events.
Th e Horologium consists of 3 elements:
A 12 Sectors, each one featuring a Zodiac Sign
B Minute Hand

Th e Horologium functions similarly to a classic analog clock, 
the Minute Hand advances from Sector to Sector indicating a 
Roman numeral on its circumference. 
When the Minute Hand reaches a Sector occupied by at least 
one Activation Token, it stops, and all Models that have their 
tokens within that Sector become active (see Hero Activation 
and Enemy Activation).
Once activated, a Model must perform its own activation. 
Th is process continues until the Mission is completed, as 
indicated in one of the Mission Cards.

Side Missions
Some Event cards may allow you to play Side Missions.
Side Missions follow these rules
◆ If you draw one of these Event Cards during a Travel 
Phase, you may decide whether to accept or reject it. If 
you decide to accept it, mark the title of the Mission in the 
Active Side Missions section of the Campaign Sheet and 
keep the Event Card aside.
◆ You can accept more than one Side Mission during a single 
Travel Phase, but you can only have up to 3 Side Missions 
Active at the same time. If you already have 3 Active Side 
Missions you may decide which one to keep before ending 
the Travel Phase.
◆ If you reject a Side Mission put its Card back into the 
Event Card Deck at the end of the Travel Phase.
◆ During a Setup Phase you may decide to play one of your 
Active Side Missions instead of the intended Campaign 
Mission (marked on the Campaign Sheet in the Campaign 
Missions section).
◆ Once the Side Mission is complete, perform a Rest Phase 
in the Town/Village indicated in the next scheduled 
Campaign Mission.
◆ It is never possible to play 2 Side Missions consecutively, 
so aft er playing any Side Mission, the next mission to be 
played must be a Campaign Mission.
Th ere are 3 types of Side Missions:
◆ Hunt Mission.
◆ Encounter Mission.
◆ Exploration Mission.
Only one Hunt Mission is present in the core game. Encounter 
and Exploration Missions and other Hunt Missions are 
introduced with the Nova Aetas Renaissance expansions.
Related Topics: Event Cards, Travel Phase (Campaign), Hunt 
Missions.

Th is section of the Rulebook explains how to prepare and 
play a Nova Aetas Renaissance Campaign. 
To start a Campaign, it is best to proceed as outlined 
below in the First Steps chapter, where the Heroes 
are selected and the Campaign Sheet fi lled out.
Th e 4 steps that make up a single Mission are explained next:
◆ Travel Phase, in which the party of Heroes, if necessary, 
moves around Italy with the possibility of making 
unexpected encounters.
◆ Setup Phase, in which the table is set up for the Mission 
and Equipment and Skills are selected for the Heroes.
◆ Mission Phase, in which Heroes will take part in the 
Mission by following the directions of Mission Cards and 
the Plotbook, gain  and Reagents, and fi nd out what 
Mission they will face next.
◆ Rest Phase, settles in the Town or Village where the 
current Mission takes place and allows the party to 
upgrade and visit various Shops.
To prepare the Nova Aetas Renaissance Campaign, proceed 
by following these steps:
◆ Make a photocopy of the Campaign Sheet, found at the 
end of this Rulebook, or print it out by downloading the 
fi le from our WebApp at: app.ludusmagnusstudio.com
◆ Each player chooses a Hero to use for the duration of the 
Campaign. Th ey take the Hero's Model, their Basic Class 
Hero Sheet, their Basic Class Skill Sheets, their Basic Class 
Starting Equipment, and their Destiny Cards.
◆ All components related to Advanced Hero Classes need 
not be taken at this time, but all players can freely consult 
them to better plan the growth of their Heroes.
◆ Nova Aetas Renaissance is balanced to be approached 
with 4 Heroes. If there are fewer players you may decide 
to use multiple Heroes each or hire Companions (easier to 
use but will not progress).
◆ Should a player want to join the game in the middle of the 
Campaign they can use one of the Heroes by "building" 
it from scratch, spending the same amount of Experience 
Points accumulated so far by the party. In this case, no new 
equipment can be craft ed (you will have to wait for the 
First Steps

Acquiring a new Class Skill
During a Rest Phase, a Hero may purchase new Skills from 
those available to them. When a Hero switches to one of the 
two Advanced Classes, they may acquire and use the Skills 
indicated on both the Basic Class Skill Sheet and the chosen 
Advanced Class.
To acquire a Skill, the player spends the  indicated by the 
cost of the Skill and writes on the Campaign Sheet the name 
of the acquired Skill, also updating the amount of  spent. 
Some Skills have a Prerequisite, so they can only be acquired 
if you have the Skill listed as a Prerequisite. 
During a single Rest Phase you can acquire as many Class 
Skills as you want as long as you have  available.
Th ere is no limit to the number of Skills a Hero can acquire 
during a Campaign.
Crafting Equipment
If at least one Hero visits the Artisan's Shop during the Rest 
Phase, they can Craft Equipment using Formulae Cards.
As long as Reagents are available, a single Hero can Craft as 
many Equipment as they wish, not only for themselves but 
also for all other Heroes; they can also create the Formulae￾type Advanced Equipment of any Advanced Class, not just 
their own.
To Craft an Equipment follow these steps:
1. Place a Hero in the Artisan's Shop.
2. Choose one or more cards from the Formulae deck or 
from the Advanced Equipment of any Hero.
3. Delete the Reagents and Florins required by the chosen 
Formulae Cards from the Campaign Sheet.
4. Mark the newly craft ed Equipment on the Campaign 
Sheet. From this moment on it will be available to the 
Heroes.
Keep in mind that you can only craft an Equipment a 
number of times equal to the number of Formulae Cards that 
represent it. So, if an Equipment has only one Formulae Card 
and is already marked on the Campaign Sheet, you will not 
be able to craft it again.
If an Equipment is sold or discarded (for example, if it is a 
Potion), it must be deleted from the Campaign Sheet, and it 
can be craft ed again.
