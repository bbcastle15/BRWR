CONTENT S
Forgotten for centuries, ancient catacombs are rediscovered 
beneath the cities of Renaissance Italy. Many rush to explore 
them, searching for ancient treasures and lost knowledge, 
but no one imagines what lies hidden in the dark tunnels 
of these mysterious places. Among the ancient rooms and 
maze-like corridors, an evil force has awakened, and with 
it the cadaverous guardians of these cursed places. It will be 
your task to face this new enemy before its damned forces 
emerge from the darkness of the catacombs to bring their 
curse to those who live on the surface!

How to use this Expansion
 is Expansion adds a Side Campaign that can be 
incorporated into any Main Campaign of Nova Aetas 
Renaissance from the Core Game or the Vesuvius 
Expansion.  is Side Campaign consists of 2 Side Missions 
and 1 Hunt Mission that must be played in sequence, 
starting with Mission CT1 - “Into Darkness.” If you decide 
to use the Catacombs Expansion in your Campaign, add the 
Event Card found in this box to the deck of Event Cards 
you are using in your Campaign. If you draw the “Into the 
Unknown” Event Card during a Travel Phase, you may 
choose to undertake it as a normal Side Mission. Once you 
have undertaken this Side Campaign, you must play the 3 
Side Missions that comprise it consecutively, starting with 
Mission CT1 - “Into Darkness.”
In addition, this expansion adds Expedition mode, which 
allows you to play new Side Misions to explore randomly 
generated Catacombs during a main campaign in the Core 
Game or Vesuvius Expansion. Finally, Catacombs adds a 
new Hero that can be played in any Campaign.
At the end of Missions CT1 and CT2 - “Back re” you may 
wish to quit the Side Campaign. In this case, return the 
“Into the Unknown” Event Card to the Event Card deck
